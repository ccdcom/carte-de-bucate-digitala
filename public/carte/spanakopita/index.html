Spanakopita

Spinach Pie
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Spinach pie is one of the oldest pleasures of the Greek kitchen, a cousin of the Balkan and Anatolian pies made with layered dough. In the countryside it was made with whatever greens the hills offered: spinach, chard, dill, fennel fronds, wild sorrel.

Feta brings salt and richness, herbs bring freshness, and the golden phyllo shatters at the first bite. It is eaten warm or cold, for lunch, at picnics and during Lent in a version without cheese.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

The Village Tray Pie

Serves 8 · 1½ hours
A big tray of spinach, herbs and feta between crisp sheets of phyllo.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 lb fresh spinach (or 1 lb frozen, thawed and squeezed)
  • 1 bunch scallions and 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 cup chopped dill and ½ cup chopped parsley
  • 10 oz feta, crumbled
  • 3 eggs
  • ½ cup olive oil, plus more for brushing
  • Salt, pepper and a little grated nutmeg
  • 1 lb phyllo dough, thawed

METHOD

  1. Wash and chop the spinach. Wilt it in a large pot with a pinch of salt, then squeeze out as much water as you can.
  2. Soften the onion and scallions in 3 tablespoons of olive oil for 6–8 minutes.
  3. Mix the spinach, onions, herbs, feta and eggs. Season with pepper and nutmeg (go easy on salt; feta is salty).
  4. Heat the oven to 350 °F. Oil a 9 × 13-inch baking dish and lay in half the phyllo sheets, brushing each with oil.
  5. Spread the filling evenly, then cover with the remaining sheets, brushing each. Tuck in the edges.
  6. Score the top into squares with a sharp knife, sprinkle with a few drops of water and bake for 50–60 minutes, until deep golden.
  7. Rest 15 minutes before cutting.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Spanakopita, Wild Greens and Brown Butter

Serves 4 · 2 hours
A crisp phyllo cylinder of wild greens and sheep’s cheese, with a spinach velouté, feta snow and lemon.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 lb mixed tender greens (spinach, chard, sorrel, wild fennel)
  • 2 scallions, 2 tbsp dill, 1 tbsp mint, finely chopped
  • 4 oz sheep’s feta and 2 oz kefalograviera, grated
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 8 sheets phyllo and ½ cup clarified butter
  • Velouté: 8 oz baby spinach, 1 shallot, ½ cup vegetable stock, ¼ cup cream, 1 tbsp butter
  • Feta snow: 2 oz feta, frozen
  • Preserved lemon, dill oil, wild fennel fronds

TECHNIQUE

  1. Wilt the greens, squeeze very dry and chop. Mix with the scallions, herbs, cheeses and yolk. Season.
  2. Brush a phyllo sheet with clarified butter, stack 2 sheets and roll the filling inside into a tight cylinder 1¼ inches thick. Make 4 and chill 20 minutes.
  3. Bake at 375 °F for 18–20 minutes, turning once, until evenly golden.
  4. Velouté: soften the shallot in butter, add the stock and cream, bring to a boil, add the spinach for 30 seconds and blend immediately until bright green. Strain and season.
  5. Finely dice the preserved lemon peel.
A pool of bright green velouté, the cylinder sliced in two on the bias and set upright, frozen feta grated over like snow, a few dots of preserved lemon, dill oil and fennel fronds.
