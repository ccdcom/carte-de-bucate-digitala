🇨🇿 Czechia

Svíčková

Czech Braised Beef
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Svíčková na smetaně is the Czech festive dish: beef sirloin marinated and roasted with root vegetables, then served with a smooth, golden sauce of puréed vegetables and cream. It descends from the aristocratic kitchens of the Habsburg Empire and became the Sunday lunch and wedding meal of Czech families.

It is always served the same way: slices of beef covered in sauce, bread dumplings (knedlíky), a spoon of cranberry sauce, a slice of lemon and a swirl of whipped cream.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Grandmother’s Sunday Svíčková

Serves 6 · 3½ hours, plus overnight marinating
Beef braised on root vegetables, the vegetables puréed with cream into a velvety sauce.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2½ lb beef sirloin or top round
  • 3 oz bacon, cut into strips (for larding, optional)
  • 2 carrots, 1 parsnip, ¼ celery root, 1 large onion, all diced
  • 10 peppercorns, 5 allspice berries, 2 bay leaves, 1 tsp thyme
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3 tbsp white vinegar and 1 tbsp sugar
  • 2 cups beef stock
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tsp mustard and lemon juice
  • To serve: bread dumplings, cranberry sauce, lemon slices, whipped cream

METHOD

  1. If you like, lard the beef with bacon strips. Season and cover with the vegetables and spices; refrigerate overnight.
  2. Brown the beef in the butter in a roasting pan. Add the vegetables and brown them, then add the vinegar, sugar and stock.
  3. Cover and braise at 325 °F for 2½ hours, until tender, adding stock if needed.
  4. Lift out the meat. Remove the bay leaves and some of the whole spices, then blend the vegetables with the cooking liquid until smooth.
  5. Stir in the cream and simmer until velvety. Season with mustard, lemon, salt and sugar: the sauce should be gently sweet and sour.
  6. Slice the beef and serve with plenty of sauce, dumplings, a spoon of cranberries, a lemon slice and whipped cream.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Svíčková, Tenderloin and Root

Serves 4 · 4 hours
Roasted beef tenderloin, a silky root-vegetable cream sauce, bread dumpling crisp, cranberry gel and a cultured cream.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1¼ lb center-cut beef tenderloin, marinated with vegetables and spices for 12 hours
  • Sauce: 2 carrots, 1 parsnip, ½ celery root, 1 onion, roasted until caramelized, 2 cups veal stock, ¾ cup cream, lemon, mustard
  • Bread dumpling: 1 dumpling, sliced thin, toasted in butter until crisp
  • Cranberry gel: 1 cup cranberry juice, 2 tbsp sugar, ½ tsp agar-agar
  • Cultured cream: ½ cup crème fraîche, lemon zest
  • Glazed baby carrots and parsnips, lemon zest, chervil

TECHNIQUE

  1. Roast the vegetables until deeply caramelized, simmer with the stock, then blend with the cream until silky. Season with lemon and mustard and pass through a sieve.
  2. Sear the tenderloin and roast at 275 °F to 130 °F internal. Rest 10 minutes.
  3. Make the cranberry gel.
  4. Glaze the baby vegetables. Toast the dumpling slices.
Two thick slices of tenderloin over a generous pool of sauce, glazed baby vegetables, a crisp dumpling slice, dots of cranberry gel, a quenelle of cultured cream with lemon zest and chervil.
