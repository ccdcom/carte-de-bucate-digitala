🇭🇺 Hungary

Duck à la Hortobágy

Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Hortobágyi palacsinta, named after the great Hungarian puszta where herdsmen roamed, is a savory crêpe filled with meat stewed in paprika (pörkölt), rolled, covered in a paprika and sour cream sauce and baked. It was made famous by Hungarian restaurants in the 1950s and 60s.

In Budapest’s modern dining rooms it is reimagined with duck confit wrapped in a silky crêpe, with the same paprika sauce, a refined dish that keeps the taste of the puszta.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Hortobágy Pancakes

Serves 6 (12 crêpes) · 2 hours
Crêpes filled with paprika-stewed meat, rolled and baked in a sour cream sauce.

INGREDIENTS

  • Crêpes: 1½ cups flour, 2 eggs, 1½ cups milk, ½ cup sparkling water, pinch of salt, oil
  • Filling: 1½ lb chicken thighs or veal, diced, 2 onions, chopped, 2 tbsp lard, 2 tbsp sweet paprika, 1 tomato, 1 green pepper, salt
  • Sauce: 1 cup sour cream, 1 tbsp flour, the cooking juices from the stew
  • Chopped parsley

METHOD

  1. Make a smooth batter, rest 30 minutes and fry 12 thin crêpes in an oiled 9-inch pan.
  2. Cook the onions in lard until golden, stir in the paprika off the heat, then add the meat, tomato, pepper and salt with a little water. Cover and stew for 45 minutes, until tender.
  3. Lift the meat out of the sauce and chop it finely. Mix with 2–3 tablespoons of the sour cream.
  4. Fill each crêpe with meat, fold in the sides and roll up tightly, like a spring roll. Arrange in a buttered baking dish.
  5. Whisk the remaining sour cream with the flour into the stew juices, simmer 2 minutes and pour over the crêpes.
  6. Bake at 375 °F for 15 minutes. Sprinkle with parsley and serve hot.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Duck Confit Palacsinta

Serves 4 · 1 day
Duck leg confit in a thin crêpe, seared crisp, with a paprika and sour cream velouté, cucumber and pickled pepper.

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 duck legs, cured overnight with salt, garlic and thyme, then confit in duck fat at 250 °F for 3 hours
  • 1 shallot, 1 tsp sweet paprika, 2 tbsp reduced duck jus
  • 8 thin crêpes (classic batter, made thinner)
  • Paprika velouté: 1 shallot, 2 tsp sweet paprika, 1 cup chicken stock, ½ cup sour cream, 1 tbsp butter
  • Cucumber salad: 1 cucumber, finely sliced, with sour cream and dill
  • Pickled wax pepper rings, paprika oil, chives

TECHNIQUE

  1. Shred the confit meat and mix with the softened shallot, paprika and duck jus.
  2. Roll the duck tightly in the crêpes into neat cylinders, then trim the ends.
  3. Velouté: soften the shallot, add the paprika off the heat, then the stock; simmer 10 minutes, blend with the sour cream and butter and strain.
  4. Sear the rolls in a little duck fat until crisp and golden on all sides; cut each on the bias.
Two bias-cut crisp rolls on a pool of paprika velouté, a small heap of cucumber salad, pickled pepper rings, drops of paprika oil and chives.
