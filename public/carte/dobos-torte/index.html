Dobos Torte

Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

In 1884 the Budapest confectioner József C. Dobos created a cake for the age before refrigeration: thin sponge layers filled with a stable chocolate buttercream and topped with a hard caramel lid that kept it from drying out. It traveled well and kept for days.

Shown at the 1885 National General Exhibition, it delighted Emperor Franz Joseph and Empress Elisabeth and spread through the grand cafés of the Austro-Hungarian Empire. Dobos later gave his recipe to the Budapest Pastry and Gingerbread Makers’ Guild.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

The Budapest Café Torte

Serves 12 · 3 hours, plus chilling
Six thin sponge layers, chocolate buttercream and a crown of caramel wedges.

INGREDIENTS

  • Sponge: 6 eggs, separated, ¾ cup sugar, 1 cup flour, 1 tsp vanilla
  • Buttercream: 4 eggs, ¾ cup sugar, 5 oz dark chocolate, melted, 1 cup + 2 tbsp butter, soft
  • Caramel: ¾ cup sugar, 1 tbsp butter, a few drops of lemon juice
  • Chopped toasted hazelnuts for the sides

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 400 °F. Draw six 9-inch circles on parchment.
  2. Beat the yolks with half the sugar and vanilla until pale. Beat the whites with the rest of the sugar to stiff peaks. Fold together, then fold in the flour.
  3. Spread the batter thinly into the 6 circles and bake each for 6–8 minutes, until light golden. Peel off the paper while warm.
  4. Buttercream: whisk the eggs and sugar over simmering water until hot and thick (160 °F). Beat until cool, add the chocolate, then beat in the butter a little at a time.
  5. Choose the best layer for the top. Stack the other 5 with buttercream between them and on the top and sides. Press hazelnuts onto the sides.
  6. Caramel: cook the sugar with the lemon juice to deep amber, stir in the butter and pour quickly over the reserved layer. Spread and, before it hardens, cut into 12 wedges with an oiled knife.
  7. Set the caramel wedges on top of the cake at an angle, each on a rosette of buttercream. Chill for 2 hours.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Dobos, Caramel Mirror

Serves 8 · 1 day
A slim rectangle of eight fine layers, dark chocolate and vanilla creams, a perfect caramel mirror and hazelnut praline.

INGREDIENTS

  • Sponge: classic recipe, baked in thin sheets
  • Chocolate cream: the classic buttercream made with 70% chocolate
  • Vanilla cream: ½ cup heavy cream whipped with 4 oz mascarpone and vanilla bean
  • Caramel: ½ cup isomalt or sugar, 2 tbsp glucose
  • Hazelnut praline paste and toasted hazelnuts
  • Gold leaf (optional), vanilla ice cream

TECHNIQUE

  1. Bake the sponge sheets and cut 8 identical rectangles.
  2. Layer, alternating chocolate cream with a few vanilla cream layers. Press lightly, chill until firm and trim into slim bars.
  3. Cook the caramel to light amber, pour very thin onto a silicone mat and, while still warm, cut neat rectangles the size of the bars.
  4. Place the caramel mirrors on top of the bars just before serving.
A slim bar with its gleaming caramel lid, a line of praline paste, three toasted hazelnuts, a quenelle of vanilla ice cream and a fleck of gold.
