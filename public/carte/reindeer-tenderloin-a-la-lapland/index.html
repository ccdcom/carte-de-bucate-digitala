🇸🇪 Sweden

Reindeer Tenderloin à la Lapland

Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

In Sweden’s far north, the Sámi have herded reindeer for centuries, following the animals’ migrations across the tundra. Reindeer meat is naturally lean and finely grained, with a gentle game flavor shaped by the wild diet of lichen and herbs.

In Lapland it is eaten in many ways, smoked, dried or thinly sliced and sautéed (renskav), always with lingonberries, the berry that has sustained northern communities since medieval times. The refined version pan-sears the tenderloin and adds a juniper jus.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Renskav, Lapland Style

Serves 4 · 30 minutes
Thinly sliced reindeer sautéed with mushrooms and cream, with mashed potato and lingonberries.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 lb frozen reindeer meat, shaved paper-thin (renskav), or thinly sliced venison or beef
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 6 juniper berries, crushed
  • 1 cup heavy cream and ½ cup beef stock
  • 1 oz brown goat cheese (brunost) or 1 tsp soy sauce (optional, for depth)
  • Salt, pepper; mashed potatoes, lingonberry jam and pickled cucumber to serve

METHOD

  1. Fry the shaved meat in butter over high heat in batches until browned. Set aside.
  2. Cook the onion and mushrooms in the same pan for 8 minutes.
  3. Return the meat, add the juniper, stock and cream, and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Stir in the brown cheese (or soy) until melted. Season.
  5. Serve with mashed potatoes, lingonberries and pickled cucumber.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Reindeer, Juniper and Lingonberry

Serves 4 · 2 hours
Pan-seared reindeer tenderloin, lingonberry gastrique, celeriac purée, charred forest mushrooms and juniper jus.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1¼ lb reindeer (or venison) tenderloin
  • Lingonberry gastrique: 2 tbsp sugar, 2 tbsp red wine vinegar, ½ cup lingonberries
  • Celeriac purée: 1 small celeriac, milk, butter
  • 6 oz forest mushrooms (chanterelles, porcini), charred in a hot pan
  • Juniper jus: 1 cup game stock, ½ cup red wine, 8 juniper berries, 1 shallot, thyme, 1 tbsp cold butter
  • Pickled shallot, thyme, flaky salt

TECHNIQUE

  1. Reduce the wine with the shallot, thyme and crushed juniper, add the stock and reduce to a glossy jus. Strain and finish with butter.
  2. Cook the celeriac in milk until soft, blend with butter until silky.
  3. Gastrique: caramelize the sugar, add the vinegar and lingonberries and simmer for 2 minutes.
  4. Season the tenderloin and sear on all sides in butter, then roast at 350 °F to 125 °F internal (rare is best for reindeer). Rest 8 minutes and slice thickly.
  5. Char the mushrooms in a dry hot pan and finish with butter.
A swoosh of celeriac purée, thick slices of rosy tenderloin, charred mushrooms, a spoon of lingonberry gastrique, pickled shallot rings and thyme, with juniper jus poured at the table.
