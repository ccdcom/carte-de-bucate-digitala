🇳🇴 Norway

Multekrem

Cloudberry Cream
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Cloudberries grow in marshes and mountain bogs and are picked by hand in late summer, often with sturdy boots and a lot of patience. Their golden-orange color earned them the name “the gold of the mountains,” and their taste is sweet, tart and slightly floral.

Multekrem, cloudberries folded into lightly sweetened whipped cream, is a Norwegian Christmas dessert, served with crisp krumkaker cookies. Sweden spoons cloudberry jam over waffles, Finland over warm bread cheese.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Christmas Multekrem

Serves 4 · 10 minutes
Cloudberries folded into softly whipped cream, with crisp krumkaker.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1–2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 cup cloudberries (fresh, thawed or cloudberry jam), plus extra to serve
  • Krumkaker (Norwegian waffle cookies), ginger snaps or crisp butter cookies

METHOD

  1. Whip the cream with the sugar to soft peaks.
  2. Gently fold in the cloudberries (if using jam, reduce or skip the sugar).
  3. Spoon into glasses and top with a few extra berries.
  4. Serve chilled with krumkaker.
Tip. Cloudberry jam is sold in Scandinavian food shops; golden raspberries or a good apricot jam can stand in.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Cloudberry, Cream and Birch

Serves 4 · 2 hours
Cloudberries fresh and as a gel, a vanilla cream, a brown-butter krumkake and birch syrup.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 cup cloudberries (fresh or frozen)
  • Cloudberry gel: ½ cup cloudberry purée, 2 tbsp sugar, ¼ tsp agar-agar
  • Vanilla cream: ¾ cup heavy cream, ¼ cup sour cream, 1 tbsp sugar, vanilla bean
  • Krumkake: 1 egg, ¼ cup sugar, 3 tbsp brown butter, ⅓ cup flour, cardamom
  • Birch syrup (or maple syrup), sorrel or lemon balm

TECHNIQUE

  1. Make the gel: boil the purée with sugar and agar, set and blend smooth.
  2. Whip the cream with the sour cream, sugar and vanilla to soft peaks.
  3. Cook the krumkake batter thin in a krumkake iron (or bake thin rounds at 350 °F for 6 minutes) and roll into cones or curves while warm.
A cloud of vanilla cream in a chilled bowl, fresh cloudberries and dots of cloudberry gel on top, a curved brown-butter krumkake, a thread of birch syrup and a few sorrel leaves.
