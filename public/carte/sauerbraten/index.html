🇩🇪 Germany

Sauerbraten

German Marinated Roast
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Sauerbraten, “sour roast,” is one of Germany’s national dishes: beef marinated for days in vinegar, wine and spices, then braised slowly. The marinade began as a way to preserve and tenderize tough meat; legend credits Charlemagne, but the method is simply very old.

Each region has its style. In the Rhineland the sauce is sweetened with raisins and Aachen gingerbread (Printen), and horse meat was once traditional. It is served with red cabbage and potato dumplings or spätzle.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Rhineland Sunday Roast

Serves 6 · 3½ hours, plus 3 days marinating
Beef marinated for three days, then braised in a sweet-sour sauce thickened with gingerbread.

INGREDIENTS

  • 3 lb beef bottom round or chuck roast
  • Marinade: 2 cups red wine, 1 cup red wine vinegar, 1 cup water, 1 onion, 1 carrot, 1 celery stalk, 2 bay leaves, 6 juniper berries, 6 cloves, 10 peppercorns, 1 tsp mustard seeds, 1 tbsp sugar
  • 3 tbsp lard or oil
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 oz gingerbread or Lebkuchen, crumbled
  • ¼ cup raisins
  • Salt, sugar to taste
  • To serve: red cabbage and potato dumplings or spätzle

METHOD

  1. Bring the marinade ingredients to a boil, cool completely, pour over the beef in a non-reactive container and refrigerate for 3 days, turning daily.
  2. Lift out the beef, pat dry and season. Strain the marinade, keeping the vegetables.
  3. Brown the beef well on all sides in the lard in a heavy pot. Remove. Brown the vegetables, stir in the tomato paste.
  4. Return the beef, pour in 3 cups of the marinade, cover and braise at 325 °F for 2½–3 hours, until tender.
  5. Lift out the meat. Strain the sauce, add the gingerbread and raisins and simmer, stirring, until thick and glossy. Season with salt and a little sugar.
  6. Slice the beef and serve with the sauce, red cabbage and dumplings.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Sauerbraten, Short Rib and Spice

Serves 4 · 4 days
Marinated and slow-cooked short rib, a glossy gingerbread jus, red cabbage gel and purée, and buttered spätzle.

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 bone-in beef short ribs
  • Marinade: as for the classic, with a little less vinegar
  • Jus: the reduced marinade, 2 cups veal stock, 1 oz Aachen Printen or gingerbread, raisins soaked in brandy
  • Red cabbage: ½ head braised with apple, red wine and cassis; half blended into purée, half set as a gel with agar
  • Spätzle: 1½ cups flour, 3 eggs, ⅓ cup milk, nutmeg, brown butter
  • Pickled red onion, juniper salt

TECHNIQUE

  1. Marinate the short ribs for 3 days.
  2. Sear and braise them at 285 °F for 5 hours, until spoon-tender. Lift out the bones; press the meat lightly and trim into neat blocks.
  3. Reduce the braising liquid with the stock and gingerbread to a glossy jus. Strain and add the brandied raisins.
  4. Make the cabbage purée and gel.
  5. Press the spätzle batter through a spätzle press into boiling water, drain and toss in brown butter.
  6. Lacquer the short rib in the jus.
A lacquered short rib block, a spoon of buttered spätzle, a swoosh of red cabbage purée, cubes of cabbage gel, pickled onion petals and raisins in the jus, finished with a pinch of juniper salt.
