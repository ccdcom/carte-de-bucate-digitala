🇳🇴 Norway

Creamy Mushroom Soup with Rømme

Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

For generations, Norwegians have walked into the autumn forests to gather mushrooms: chanterelles, porcini, hedgehog mushrooms. Under the right of public access (allemannsretten), the forest belongs to everyone who respects it.

Rømme, Norway’s thick cultured sour cream, gives this soup its gentle tang, a taste of the old farm culture where cream was kept in cool cellars until it soured. Kremet soppsuppe med rømme is forest and farm in one bowl.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Cabin Mushroom Soup

Serves 4 · 40 minutes
Wild or cultivated mushrooms, onion and stock, finished with rømme and served with flatbread.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 lb mixed mushrooms (chanterelles, cremini, porcini if you can), sliced
  • ½ oz dried porcini, soaked in 1 cup hot water
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion and 1 garlic clove, chopped
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 4 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • 1 sprig thyme
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • ½ cup rømme or thick sour cream
  • Salt, pepper, a few drops of lemon juice, chives

METHOD

  1. Melt the butter and cook the onion and garlic for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the fresh mushrooms and cook over high heat for 8 minutes, until browned. Keep a few aside for garnish.
  3. Stir in the flour, then the stock, the dried porcini with their strained soaking water, and the thyme. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Blend part or all of the soup, depending on how chunky you like it. Add the cream and heat through.
  5. Season with salt, pepper and lemon juice.
  6. Serve with a spoon of rømme, the reserved mushrooms and chives, and crisp flatbread.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Forest Floor

Serves 4 · 2 hours
A deep mushroom velouté poured over roasted chanterelles, rømme, pickled mushrooms, toasted hazelnut and spruce oil.

INGREDIENTS

  • Velouté: 1 lb cremini, roasted, ½ oz dried porcini, 1 shallot, 3 cups mushroom stock, ½ cup cream, 2 tbsp butter
  • 6 oz small chanterelles, roasted in butter
  • Pickled mushrooms: 2 oz small mushrooms or enoki in a hot brine of vinegar, sugar and juniper
  • ½ cup rømme, seasoned with lemon zest
  • Toasted hazelnuts, crushed
  • Spruce or pine oil (young needles blended with oil and strained), wood sorrel

TECHNIQUE

  1. Roast the cremini at 425 °F until dark. Sweat the shallot, add the roasted and dried mushrooms and stock, simmer 30 minutes, then blend with the cream and butter until silky. Pass through a fine sieve.
  2. Pickle the small mushrooms for at least 30 minutes.
  3. Roast the chanterelles in foaming butter.
In a warm bowl: a quenelle of rømme, roasted chanterelles and pickled mushrooms scattered like a forest floor, crushed hazelnuts and wood sorrel. Pour the hot velouté around at the table and finish with drops of spruce oil.
