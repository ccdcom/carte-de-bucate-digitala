French Onion Soup

Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Onion soup is as old as French peasant cooking, but its modern form, soupe à l’oignon gratinée, belongs to Paris and to Les Halles, the great central market, where workers, porters and night owls ate it at dawn to warm up.

The method is all about patience: onions cooked slowly for an hour until deep brown and sweet, a rich beef broth, a slice of toasted bread and a thick blanket of melted Gruyère or Comté, bubbling under the broiler.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Les Halles at Dawn

Serves 4 · 1¾ hours
Deeply caramelized onions, beef broth and a crusty cheese lid.

INGREDIENTS

  • 3 lb yellow onions, thinly sliced
  • 4 tbsp butter and 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp sugar and 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp flour
  • 1 cup dry white wine
  • 6 cups beef stock
  • 2 sprigs thyme and 1 bay leaf
  • 1 tbsp Cognac (optional)
  • 4 thick slices of baguette, toasted
  • 2 cups grated Gruyère or Comté

METHOD

  1. Melt the butter with the oil in a heavy pot. Add the onions, salt and sugar and cook over medium-low heat for 50–60 minutes, stirring often, until deep golden brown and jammy.
  2. Sprinkle in the flour and stir for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the wine, scrape the bottom, then the stock, thyme and bay leaf. Simmer for 30 minutes. Add the Cognac and season.
  4. Ladle into ovenproof bowls, float a slice of toast on each and cover generously with cheese.
  5. Broil until bubbling and browned in spots, 3–5 minutes. Serve very hot.
Tip. If the onions start to stick, add a splash of water and scrape: the brown bits are where the flavor is.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Onion, Five Ways

Serves 4 · 5 hours
A clear, intensely flavored onion consommé poured over onion petals, onion jam, a Comté-gratinated crouton and crisp shallots.

INGREDIENTS

  • Consommé: 4 lb onions, caramelized for 2 hours, 2 quarts beef stock, 1 cup dry sherry or Madeira, thyme, clarified with 3 egg whites
  • Onion jam: 2 onions, cooked down with butter, 1 tbsp sherry vinegar, 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • Onion petals: 4 small cipollini or red onions, halved, charred cut side down and roasted
  • Crisp shallots: 2 shallots, thinly sliced, fried
  • Croutons: 4 small rounds of brioche, toasted, topped with 2-year Comté and gratinated
  • Chive oil, thyme flowers

TECHNIQUE

  1. Cook the onions very slowly until mahogany brown. Add the sherry, stock and thyme and simmer 1 hour. Strain, pressing hard.
  2. Clarify the cold broth with whisked egg whites, bring slowly to a simmer, let the raft form and simmer 30 minutes. Strain through a cloth. Season.
  3. Make the jam, the charred onion petals and the crisp shallots.
  4. Top the brioche rounds with Comté and gratinate under the broiler.
In a warm wide bowl: a spoon of onion jam, three charred onion petals, the Comté crouton, crisp shallots and thyme flowers. At the table, pour the clear, hot consommé around and add a few drops of chive oil.
