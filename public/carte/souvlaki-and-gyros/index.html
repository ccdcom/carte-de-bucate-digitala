🇬🇷 Greece

Souvlaki and Gyros

Traditional Street Food
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Meat on skewers over fire is as old as Greece itself: Homer describes it. Souvlaki, little skewers of pork or chicken, became the street food of every Greek town in the 20th century, sold from small grills called souvlatzidika.

Gyros arrived later, brought by Greeks from Asia Minor in the 1920s, a cousin of the Turkish döner: meat stacked on a vertical spit and shaved as it roasts. Wrapped in a warm pita with tomato, onion, tzatziki and fries, both are the taste of a Greek night out.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Pork Souvlaki in Pita

Serves 4 (8 skewers) · 45 minutes, plus 2 hours marinating
Marinated pork skewers, homemade tzatziki and warm pita, as on any Athens street corner.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 lb boneless pork shoulder, in 1-inch cubes
  • Marinade: ¼ cup olive oil, juice of 1 lemon, 3 garlic cloves, crushed, 2 tsp dried oregano, 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper
  • Tzatziki: 1½ cups Greek yogurt, 1 cucumber, grated and squeezed, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp vinegar, dill
  • 8 pita breads
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced, 1 red onion, sliced, parsley
  • Sweet paprika and french fries (optional)

METHOD

  1. Mix the pork with the marinade and refrigerate for 2 hours or overnight.
  2. Make the tzatziki: mix the yogurt, cucumber, garlic, oil and vinegar, season and add dill. Chill.
  3. Thread the pork onto skewers (soak wooden ones first).
  4. Grill over hot charcoal or in a grill pan for 10–12 minutes, turning, until charred at the edges and cooked through.
  5. Warm the pitas on the grill with a brush of oil.
  6. Fill each pita with tzatziki, the meat slid off the skewer, tomato, onion, parsley and a few fries. Dust with paprika, roll in paper and eat at once.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Gyros, Layer by Layer

Serves 6 · 1 day
A pressed and roasted lamb and pork ‘gyros’ terrine, crisped to order, with smoked yogurt, charred pita and pickled onion.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1½ lb lamb shoulder and 1 lb pork belly, both thinly sliced
  • Marinade: ¼ cup olive oil, 4 garlic cloves, 2 tsp oregano, 1 tsp each cumin, coriander and sweet paprika, ½ tsp cinnamon, zest of 1 lemon, 2 tsp salt
  • Smoked yogurt: 1 cup strained yogurt, smoked for 10 minutes, lemon, salt
  • Cucumber: 1 cucumber, in fine ribbons, dressed with dill and vinegar
  • Pickled red onion petals
  • 6 small homemade pitas, charred
  • Sumac, olive oil, tomato powder or sweet paprika

TECHNIQUE

  1. Marinate the sliced meats overnight.
  2. Layer the slices tightly in a lined loaf pan, alternating lamb and pork. Cover, set in a water bath and bake at 300 °F for 3 hours.
  3. Weigh the terrine down with a second pan and cans, and chill overnight.
  4. Unmold and cut into thick slices. Sear on the cut sides in a very hot pan until deeply crisp, then shave thin from each slice.
  5. Season the smoked yogurt with lemon and salt.
A swoosh of smoked yogurt, a mound of crisp gyros shavings on top, cucumber ribbons and onion petals around, a dusting of sumac, olive oil and a warm torn pita on the side.
