Finnish Beef Fillet Karjala

Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

The hearty cooking of Karelia is built on slow pots: karjalanpaisti, the Karelian hot pot of beef and pork baked for hours with only salt, peppercorns and allspice, is one of Finland’s national dishes.

The refined version, Karjala naudanfilee, keeps the spirit of the region with beef and forest mushrooms: a tender fillet with a creamy mushroom and pepper sauce, a crisp potato rösti and herb-glazed baby carrots.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Karelian Hot Pot

Serves 6 · 4 hours
Beef and pork baked slowly with onions, allspice and peppercorns, with mashed potatoes and lingonberries.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1½ lb beef chuck, in 1½-inch cubes
  • 1 lb pork shoulder, in 1½-inch cubes
  • 8 oz lamb shoulder (optional, traditional), in cubes
  • 2 onions, in wedges
  • 1 tbsp allspice berries and 1 tbsp black peppercorns
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 tsp salt
  • About 2 cups water
  • Mashed potatoes, lingonberry jam and pickled beets, to serve

METHOD

  1. Layer the meats and onions in a heavy ovenproof pot, sprinkling with the salt, spices and bay leaves.
  2. Add water to just below the top of the meat.
  3. Cover and bake at 300 °F for 3–4 hours, until the meat is very tender and the juices have turned rich and brown. Check halfway and add water if needed.
  4. Serve with mashed potatoes, lingonberry jam and pickled beets.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Karjala Naudanfilee

Serves 4 · 2 hours
Gently cooked beef fillet, a creamy forest mushroom and green pepper sauce, crisp potato rösti and herb-glazed baby carrots.

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 beef tenderloin steaks (6 oz each), 1½ inches thick
  • Sauce: 6 oz chanterelles or mixed forest mushrooms, 1 shallot, 2 tbsp brandy, ¾ cup beef stock, ½ cup cream, 1 tbsp green peppercorns, 1 tsp crushed black and allspice
  • Rösti: 1 lb potatoes, grated and squeezed dry, clarified butter
  • 12 baby carrots, glazed with butter, honey, thyme and dill
  • Lingonberries, thyme, flaky salt

TECHNIQUE

  1. Make 4 small, thick rösti in rings in clarified butter until golden and crisp; keep warm.
  2. Glaze the carrots in butter, a little water and honey until tender and shiny; add dill.
  3. Season the steaks and sear in a very hot pan, then roast at 275 °F to 128 °F internal for medium-rare. Rest 8 minutes.
  4. In the same pan, sauté the mushrooms and shallot, flambé with brandy, add the stock and reduce by half, then the cream and peppercorns; simmer until it coats a spoon.
