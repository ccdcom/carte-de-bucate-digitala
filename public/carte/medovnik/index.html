🇨🇿 Czechia

Medovník

Czech Honey Cake
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Honey cake is beloved across Central and Eastern Europe, and its most famous form, the Russian medovik, is said to have been created in the 19th century for Empress Elizabeth, wife of Tsar Alexander I. The Czechs took it to heart, and medovník is now in every Prague café.

Thin layers of honey biscuit, baked crisp, are stacked with a cream of caramelized condensed milk and butter, then left to rest until the layers turn soft and melting. The top is covered with crumbs of the same honey layers.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

The Prague Café Honey Cake

Serves 12 · 3 hours, plus overnight resting
Eight thin honey layers and a caramel cream, rested overnight until tender.

INGREDIENTS

  • Dough: ⅓ cup honey, ½ cup butter, ¾ cup sugar, 3 eggs, 1 tsp baking soda, 3½ cups all-purpose flour
  • Cream: 1 can (14 oz) dulce de leche (or condensed milk boiled in the can for 2½ hours), 1 cup soft butter, 1 cup sour cream or heavy cream whipped
  • ¾ cup walnuts, finely chopped

METHOD

  1. Heat the honey, butter and sugar in a bowl over simmering water until melted. Whisk in the eggs, then the baking soda; the mixture will foam and turn golden.
  2. Remove from the heat and mix in the flour to a soft dough. Divide into 8 pieces.
  3. Roll each piece very thin on parchment and cut a 9-inch circle, keeping the trimmings.
  4. Bake each at 350 °F for 5–6 minutes, until golden. Bake the trimmings too, then crush them into crumbs.
  5. Beat the butter until fluffy, add the dulce de leche, then fold in the sour cream and the chopped walnuts.
  6. Stack the layers with cream between each, cover the top and sides, and press the crumbs all over.
  7. Refrigerate for at least 12 hours before slicing.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Honey, Pollen and Sour Cream

Serves 8 · 2 days
A slim slice of 12-layer honey cake, buckwheat honey caramel, sour cream sorbet, bee pollen and honeycomb.

INGREDIENTS

  • Honey cake: as for the classic, with buckwheat or forest honey, rolled into 12 very thin layers
  • Cream: whipped sour cream lightly sweetened, and burnt-honey caramel folded into mascarpone
  • Honey caramel: ½ cup buckwheat honey cooked to dark amber, ⅓ cup cream, pinch of salt
  • Sour cream sorbet: 1½ cups sour cream, ½ cup water, ½ cup sugar, lemon
  • Honeycomb candy: ¾ cup sugar, 2 tbsp honey, 2 tbsp water, 1 tsp baking soda
  • Bee pollen, a piece of fresh honeycomb (optional)

TECHNIQUE

  1. Make 12 thin layers and assemble with the two creams alternately. Press lightly and rest 2 days in the fridge.
  2. Churn the sorbet.
  3. Make the caramel and the honeycomb candy; break the honeycomb into shards.
  4. Trim the cake and cut into neat slim slices.
A slice on its side showing the fine layers, a spoon of honey caramel, a quenelle of sour cream sorbet, shards of honeycomb candy, a sprinkle of bee pollen and a small piece of fresh comb.
