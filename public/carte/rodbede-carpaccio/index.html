Rødbede Carpaccio

Beet Carpaccio
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Beets (rødbeder) have been a Danish winter staple for generations, usually pickled in vinegar and sugar and served beside meatballs, liver pâté and roast pork.

The modern Danish kitchen gives the old staple a lighter touch: thin slices of red and golden beets arranged like a flower, citrus-scented oil, horseradish cream and toasted walnuts. Fresh, bright and simple.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Grandmother’s Pickled Beets

Makes 2 jars (serves 6–8) · 1½ hours, plus 1 day
Sweet-sour pickled beets, the way every Danish pantry keeps them, served sliced as a simple salad.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 lb beets, scrubbed
  • 1 cup white vinegar
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ½ cup water
  • 4 whole cloves, 1 bay leaf, 1 cinnamon stick, 6 peppercorns
  • To serve: thinly sliced red onion, a spoon of sour cream, dill

METHOD

  1. Boil the beets in salted water for 40–60 minutes, until tender. Cool, slip off the skins and slice ¼ inch thick.
  2. Bring the vinegar, sugar, water and spices to a boil until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Pack the beet slices into clean jars and pour the hot brine over them. Seal.
  4. Refrigerate at least 1 day. They keep for several weeks.
  5. Serve as a salad, fanned on a plate with red onion, a spoon of sour cream and dill, or beside roast pork and meatballs.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Beet Carpaccio, Citrus and Horseradish

Serves 4 as a starter · 2 hours
Paper-thin red and golden beets in a floral circle, citrus oil, horseradish cream, toasted walnuts and pickled beet gel.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 red beets and 2 golden beets, of similar size
  • Citrus oil: zest of 1 orange and 1 lemon warmed gently in ⅓ cup mild olive oil
  • Horseradish cream: ½ cup crème fraîche, 2 tbsp freshly grated horseradish, lemon, salt
  • Pickled beet gel: ½ cup beet pickling liquid, ½ tsp agar-agar
  • ¼ cup walnuts, toasted and crushed
  • Microgreens, chervil, sorrel, flaky sea salt

TECHNIQUE

  1. Roast the beets wrapped in foil at 400 °F for 50–60 minutes, until just tender. Peel and chill.
  2. Slice the beets as thinly as possible on a mandoline.
  3. Make the citrus oil and let it infuse for 30 minutes; strain.
  4. Boil the pickling liquid with agar, set it, then blend into a smooth gel.
  5. Whip the crème fraîche with the horseradish, lemon and salt.
Overlapping red and golden slices arranged in a full circle like a flower, brushed with citrus oil and touched with flaky salt; in the center a quenelle of horseradish cream, dots of beet gel around, crushed walnuts and a few herbs.
