🇦🇹 Austria

Tafelspitz

Served Cold in Aspic
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Tafelspitz, beef simmered gently in a fragrant broth, was the daily lunch of Emperor Franz Joseph and the pride of Vienna’s beef restaurants, where diners chose among two dozen cuts. The broth comes first, then the meat with apple-horseradish, chive sauce and fried potatoes.

Served cold in aspic, it becomes something else: slices of tender beef and bright vegetables suspended in crystal-clear jelly made from the same broth, a cool, precise dish of old Viennese refinement.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Viennese Boiled Beef with Its Sauces

Serves 6 · 3½ hours
Beef simmered for hours with root vegetables, served hot with apple-horseradish and chive sauce. Any leftovers make a fine aspic the next day.

INGREDIENTS

  • 3–4 lb beef top sirloin cap (Tafelspitz) or brisket
  • 1 lb beef bones
  • 1 onion, halved and charred cut side down in a dry pan
  • 2 carrots, 1 parsnip, ¼ celery root, 1 leek
  • 10 peppercorns, 2 bay leaves, a few juniper berries, salt
  • Apple-horseradish: 2 tart apples, grated, 2–3 tbsp grated fresh horseradish, lemon juice, pinch of sugar
  • Chive sauce: 2 slices white bread soaked in milk, 2 hard-boiled yolks, 1 raw yolk, ½ cup oil, vinegar, chives
  • Pan-fried potatoes, to serve

METHOD

  1. Blanch the bones in boiling water for 5 minutes and rinse.
  2. Put the bones and beef in a large pot, cover with cold water and bring slowly to a bare simmer. Skim well.
  3. Add the charred onion, spices and a little salt. Simmer very gently, uncovered, for 2½–3 hours. Add the vegetables for the last hour.
  4. Apple-horseradish: mix the grated apple with the horseradish, lemon juice and sugar.
  5. Chive sauce: blend the soaked bread with the yolks, then add the oil slowly like a mayonnaise; season with vinegar and salt and stir in plenty of chives.
  6. Serve the broth first as a soup, then the beef sliced against the grain with the vegetables, sauces and fried potatoes.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Tafelspitz in Crystal Aspic

Serves 8 as a starter · 2 days
Tender beef and vegetables set in a clarified consommé jelly, with apple-horseradish cream, chive emulsion and pickled root vegetables.

INGREDIENTS

  • Cooked Tafelspitz and its broth (from the classic recipe)
  • Clarification: 3 egg whites, 4 oz lean ground beef, carrot and leek trimmings
  • Powdered gelatin: about 1 tbsp per 2 cups of consommé (adjust to the broth’s natural set)
  • Vegetables: carrot, celery root and parsnip, in fine dice, blanched
  • Apple-horseradish cream: ½ cup crème fraîche, 2 tbsp grated horseradish, ½ green apple, finely diced
  • Chive emulsion: chives blended with grapeseed oil and a little yolk
  • Pickled baby carrots and beets, rye crisps, flaky salt

TECHNIQUE

  1. Clarify 4 cups of cold, defatted broth with the whisked egg whites, beef and trimmings, simmering gently for 40 minutes. Strain through a cloth.
  2. Season the consommé well (cold dulls flavor) and add the bloomed gelatin.
  3. Line a terrine mold with plastic wrap. Pour a thin layer of consommé, let it set, then arrange slices of beef and the vegetables in neat layers, covering each with consommé and letting it set before the next.
  4. Chill overnight until firm.
  5. Make the horseradish cream and chive emulsion.
A clean slice of the terrine on a cold plate, glistening and transparent, a quenelle of apple-horseradish cream, a line of chive emulsion, pickled baby vegetables, a rye crisp and a few flakes of salt.
