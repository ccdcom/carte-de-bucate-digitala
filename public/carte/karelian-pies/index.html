🇫🇮 Finland

Karelian Pies

Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Karjalanpiirakat come from Karelia, the eastern frontier region between Finland and Russia: thin, oval rye crusts with crimped edges, filled with rice porridge (or, in older times, barley or potato) and baked in a very hot oven.

They became a national treasure and are protected as a traditional specialty in the European Union. Finns eat them warm, spread with munavoi, egg butter, at breakfast, at cafés and at every family gathering.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Grandmother’s Karjalanpiirakat

Makes 20 · 2½ hours
Thin rye crusts filled with creamy rice porridge, brushed with butter-milk and served with egg butter.

INGREDIENTS

  • Rice porridge: 1 cup water, ¾ cup short-grain rice, 4 cups whole milk, 1 tsp salt, 1 tbsp butter
  • Crust: 1 cup water, 1 tsp salt, 1½ cups rye flour, ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • Dip: ½ cup milk with 3 tbsp melted butter
  • Egg butter (munavoi): 3 hard-boiled eggs, chopped, mixed with ½ cup soft butter and salt

METHOD

  1. Porridge: simmer the rice in the water until absorbed, add the milk and cook very gently, stirring often, for 40–50 minutes until thick and creamy. Add salt and butter and cool.
  2. Crust: mix the water, salt and flours into a stiff dough. Shape into a log and cut into 20 pieces.
  3. Roll each piece into a very thin oval, about 6 × 4 inches, dusting with rye flour.
  4. Spread 2 tablespoons of porridge in the center, leaving a ¾-inch border. Fold the border in over the filling and pinch it into small pleats with your fingers, leaving the center open.
  5. Bake on a lined sheet at 525–550 °F (as hot as your oven goes) for 12–15 minutes, until the edges are browned and the porridge has golden spots.
  6. Dip or brush each hot pie in the butter-milk, then stack them under a towel to soften. Serve with egg butter.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Karelia, Rye and Roe

Makes 16 small pies (serves 8 as a starter) · 3 hours
Small, crisp Karelian pies with a creamy barley and rice filling, smoked egg butter, vendace roe and dill.

INGREDIENTS

  • Crust: as in the classic recipe, rolled thinner and cut into small ovals
  • Filling: ½ cup pearl barley and ¼ cup short-grain rice cooked slowly in milk with butter and salt, finished with a little cream
  • Smoked egg butter: 3 eggs boiled for 9 minutes, chopped, mixed with ½ cup cultured butter, smoked salt and chives
  • 2 oz vendace roe (muikunmäti) or another small golden roe
  • Crème fraîche, pickled red onion, dill, brown butter for brushing

TECHNIQUE

  1. Make the filling and cool it.
  2. Roll, fill and crimp small ovals as in the classic recipe.
  3. Bake at the oven’s highest setting for 10–12 minutes and brush with brown butter.
  4. Make the smoked egg butter.
Two warm small pies per plate, a quenelle of smoked egg butter on each, a spoon of golden roe, a dot of crème fraîche, pickled onion and dill.
