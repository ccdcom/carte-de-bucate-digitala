Veal in Creamy Mushroom Sauce

Zürcher Geschnetzeltes
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Veal in creamy mushroom sauce was born in Zürich’s bourgeois kitchens in the late 19th century: thin strips of veal sautéed quickly, mushrooms, white wine, cream and a touch of lemon. Elegant, fast and unmistakably Swiss.

Households often replaced the veal with pork or chicken, and some cooks add chanterelles or a splash of Cognac. Its inseparable partner is a golden rösti.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Zürich Home Cooking

Serves 4 · 30 minutes
Veal strips, mushrooms and a creamy white wine sauce, ready in 30 minutes.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1½ lb veal (top round or tenderloin), cut into thin strips
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 3 tbsp butter and 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 shallot, finely chopped
  • 8 oz white or cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • ½ cup veal or chicken stock
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tsp lemon juice, salt, white pepper
  • Chopped parsley; rösti to serve

METHOD

  1. Dust the veal lightly with flour and season.
  2. Sear the veal in batches in very hot butter and oil for 1 minute, just until browned. Set aside.
  3. Lower the heat, add the shallot and mushrooms and cook for 5 minutes, until golden.
  4. Add the wine and reduce by half, then the stock and cream. Simmer 4–5 minutes until slightly thickened.
  5. Return the veal and its juices and warm through for 1 minute only, so it stays tender.
  6. Season with lemon, salt and pepper, sprinkle with parsley and serve with rösti.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Veal, Chanterelle and Cream

Serves 4 · 2 hours
Pink veal tenderloin, a glossy cream sauce of veal jus and Champagne, chanterelles, and a crisp rösti tower.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1¼ lb veal tenderloin, trimmed
  • Sauce: 1 cup veal jus, ½ cup Champagne or dry white wine, 1 shallot, ¾ cup cream, 1 tbsp cold butter, lemon
  • 8 oz chanterelles, cleaned
  • Rösti: 1 lb potatoes, grated, clarified butter
  • Glazed baby carrots, chervil, chive oil

TECHNIQUE

  1. Make small, tall rösti in rings and fry until golden and crisp.
  2. Reduce the Champagne with the shallot, add the jus and reduce by half, then the cream; simmer until coating a spoon. Finish with butter and lemon; strain.
  3. Sear the veal on all sides and roast at 300 °F to 135 °F internal. Rest 8 minutes, then slice into medallions and cut a few into thin strips.
  4. Sauté the chanterelles in foaming butter.
A rösti tower to one side, veal medallions and a few strips coated in sauce, chanterelles scattered, glazed carrots, chervil and a few drops of chive oil in the sauce.
