Sticky Toffee Pudding

Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Sticky toffee pudding is a modern British classic. It was popularized in the 1970s by Francis Coulson at the Sharrow Bay hotel in the Lake District, who said he had the recipe from a friend, and the village of Cartmel made it famous in shops across Britain.

A moist sponge made with chopped dates, soaked in hot toffee sauce and served with cream, custard or vanilla ice cream: it is the dessert of pubs and Sunday lunches.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

The Lake District Pudding

Serves 8 · 1 hour
A soft date sponge drenched in warm toffee sauce.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1¼ cups pitted dates, chopped
  • 1 cup boiling water and 1 tsp baking soda
  • ¼ cup butter, softened
  • ¾ cup dark brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1⅓ cups self-rising flour (or all-purpose with 1½ tsp baking powder)
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Toffee sauce: ½ cup butter, 1 cup dark brown sugar, 1 cup heavy cream, pinch of salt
  • Vanilla ice cream or custard, to serve

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 350 °F. Butter an 8-inch square dish.
  2. Pour the boiling water over the dates, add the baking soda and leave for 10 minutes.
  3. Beat the butter and sugar, then beat in the eggs one at a time. Fold in the flour, then the dates with their liquid and the vanilla. The batter will be loose.
  4. Pour into the dish and bake for 30–35 minutes, until springy.
  5. Make the sauce: melt the butter and sugar, add the cream and salt and simmer for 3 minutes until glossy.
  6. Prick the hot pudding all over and pour over a third of the sauce. Let it soak for 5 minutes.
  7. Cut into squares and serve with the rest of the sauce and ice cream or custard.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Toffee, Date and Milk

Serves 6 · 4 hours
Individual date sponges, a deep muscovado toffee, Medjool date purée, brown-butter ice cream and milk crumb.

INGREDIENTS

  • Sponge: as in the classic recipe, made with Medjool dates and a pinch of ground ginger, baked in 6 small molds
  • Toffee: ½ cup muscovado sugar, ½ cup butter, ¾ cup cream, 1 tbsp black treacle or molasses, flaky salt
  • Date purée: 6 Medjool dates, ⅓ cup water, lemon juice
  • Brown butter ice cream: ½ cup brown butter, 1½ cups milk, ½ cup cream, 4 yolks, ½ cup sugar
  • Milk crumb: ⅓ cup milk powder, 2 tbsp flour, 2 tbsp sugar, 2 tbsp melted butter
  • Crème fraîche

TECHNIQUE

  1. Make the ice cream: make a custard with the milk, cream, yolks and sugar to 180 °F, blend in the brown butter, chill and churn.
  2. Simmer the dates with the water, blend with lemon juice into a smooth purée.
  3. Bake the milk crumb at 250 °F for 15 minutes, until crisp.
  4. Bake the sponges at 350 °F for 18–20 minutes. Unmold.
  5. Make the toffee and keep it warm. Soak the warm sponges in toffee for a few minutes.
A glossy sponge, toffee spooned over until it runs down the sides, a smear of date purée, milk crumb, a quenelle of brown-butter ice cream and a small spoon of crème fraîche, with flaky salt on the toffee.
