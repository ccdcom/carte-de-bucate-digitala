Tapas

Spanish Bar Bites
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

In Andalusian taverns, a slice of bread or ham was placed over a glass of wine to keep out dust and flies: a tapa, a “lid.” Over time the lid became the point, and small plates to share turned into a way of life across Spain.

Tapas are less a recipe than a rhythm: a few bites in one bar, a few more in the next, always standing, always talking. Three classics capture the spirit: garlic shrimp sizzling in oil, crisp potatoes with spicy sauce, and the humble tomato bread of Catalonia.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

A Barcelona Bar Trio

Serves 4–6 as tapas · 1 hour
Gambas al ajillo, patatas bravas and pan con tomate: three tapas for a table of friends.

INGREDIENTS

  • Gambas: 1 lb raw shrimp, peeled, ½ cup olive oil, 6 garlic cloves, sliced, 1 dried chili (guindilla), parsley, salt
  • Bravas: 2 lb potatoes, in 1-inch cubes, olive oil for frying
  • Brava sauce: 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp flour, 1 tbsp smoked hot paprika (pimentón picante), 1 tsp sweet paprika, 1 cup chicken stock, 1 tsp sherry vinegar
  • Alioli: ½ cup mayonnaise with 1 crushed garlic clove
  • Pan con tomate: 1 rustic loaf, 3 ripe tomatoes, 1 garlic clove, olive oil, flaky salt
  • Jamón ibérico slices (optional)

METHOD

  1. Bravas: boil the potato cubes for 6 minutes, drain and dry well. Fry in olive oil at 350 °F for 6–8 minutes, until golden and crisp. Season with salt.
  2. Brava sauce: warm the oil, stir in the flour and both paprikas for 30 seconds, whisk in the stock and simmer for 5 minutes until thick. Add the vinegar and salt.
  3. Gambas: heat the oil with the garlic and chili in a small clay dish or skillet over medium heat until the garlic is just golden. Add the shrimp and salt and cook 2–3 minutes, until pink. Add parsley and serve sizzling.
  4. Pan con tomate: toast thick slices of bread, rub with the cut garlic, then with halved tomatoes until the bread is red and juicy. Drizzle with oil and add flaky salt.
  5. Serve the bravas with the sauce and alioli spooned over, the shrimp in their dish with bread to dip, and the tomato bread with jamón.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Tapas, Elevated

Serves 4 (3 bites each) · 3 hours
Three precise bites: a liquid ‘bravas’ potato, red prawn with garlic emulsion, and a crystal bread with tomato and jamón.

INGREDIENTS

  • Bravas: 8 small waxy potatoes, ¼ cup brown butter, smoked paprika oil, alioli foam (½ cup milk, 2 tbsp alioli, 1 tsp lecithin or 1 egg white)
  • Prawns: 4 large red prawns (Carabinero or Gamba roja), garlic emulsion (4 confit garlic cloves, 2 tbsp prawn-head oil, 2 tbsp olive oil), parsley oil
  • Crystal bread: 4 thin slices of very airy bread (or pan de cristal), 1 ripe tomato, grated and seasoned
  • 4 paper-thin slices of jamón ibérico de bellota
  • Flaky salt, Espelette or cayenne pepper

TECHNIQUE

  1. Bravas: cut the potatoes into neat cylinders, cook slowly in brown butter until tender, then fry until crisp. Hollow the top slightly.
  2. Make a light alioli foam by blending the warm milk with alioli and lecithin until airy.
  3. Prawns: peel, keeping the heads. Fry the heads in olive oil, crush them and strain to make a deep red oil. Blend the confit garlic with it into an emulsion.
  4. Sear the prawn tails for 30 seconds per side in a very hot pan, leaving them translucent in the center.
  5. Toast the crystal bread until glassy and spread with the grated tomato just before serving; drape with jamón.
On a long slate: the bravas cylinder topped with alioli foam and a drop of paprika oil; the prawn on a spoon of garlic emulsion with its head beside it to be sucked; the crystal bread with tomato and jamón, finished with olive oil and flaky salt.
