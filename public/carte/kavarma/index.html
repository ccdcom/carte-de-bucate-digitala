🇧🇬 Bulgaria

Kavarma

Meat and Vegetable Stew
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Kavarma is the warmth of the Bulgarian winter: pork with onions, peppers, mushrooms and wine, cooked slowly in an earthenware gyuveche until the juices turn rich and thick. Its name comes from the Turkish kavurma, meat browned in its own fat.

Every region claims its own version. Around Melnik it is made with local red wine, in the Rhodopes with mountain mushrooms, and almost everywhere it arrives at the table still bubbling, often with an egg cracked on top in the last minutes.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Clay Pot Kavarma

Serves 4 · 2 hours
Pork, onions, peppers and red wine, baked in small clay pots and served sizzling.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1½ lb pork shoulder, in ¾-inch cubes
  • 3 tbsp sunflower oil or lard
  • 2 large onions, sliced
  • 2 leeks, sliced
  • 2 red bell peppers, in strips
  • 8 oz mushrooms, quartered
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup dry red wine
  • 2 tsp sweet paprika, 1 tsp dried savory (chubritsa), 2 bay leaves, salt and pepper
  • 4 eggs (optional) and chopped parsley

METHOD

  1. Brown the pork in the oil in batches over high heat. Return all to the pan.
  2. Add the onions and leeks and cook for 10 minutes until soft. Add the peppers and mushrooms and cook 5 minutes more.
  3. Stir in the paprika, then the tomatoes, wine, savory and bay leaves. Season and simmer, covered, for 30 minutes.
  4. Divide between 4 clay pots (or one baking dish), cover with lids or foil and bake at 350 °F for 45 minutes, until the meat is very tender.
  5. If you like, crack an egg into each pot and bake uncovered for 6–8 minutes more, until the white is set.
  6. Sprinkle with parsley and serve bubbling, with bread.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Kavarma, Melnik Wine and Mushroom

Serves 4 · 5 hours
Slow-cooked pork cheek in a Melnik wine glaze, wild mushrooms, a smoked onion purée and a confit egg yolk.

INGREDIENTS

  • 8 pork cheeks, trimmed
  • 1 bottle (3 cups) Melnik or other full red wine, reduced by half
  • 2 cups dark pork or veal stock
  • 1 onion, 1 carrot, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tsp sweet paprika, savory, bay leaf
  • Smoked onion purée: 3 onions, sliced, 2 tbsp butter, ¼ cup cream, smoked salt
  • 8 oz wild mushrooms (porcini, chanterelles), cleaned
  • 4 egg yolks and 1 cup olive oil for confit
  • Pickled red pepper ribbons, savory leaves, toasted bread crumbs

TECHNIQUE

  1. Season and sear the cheeks. Add the vegetables, paprika and herbs, then the reduced wine and stock. Cover and braise at 300 °F for 3 hours, until tender.
  2. Lift out the cheeks. Strain and reduce the braising liquid to a glossy glaze.
  3. Purée: cook the onions in butter over low heat for 45 minutes until deep golden, add the cream, blend until silky and season with smoked salt.
  4. Confit the yolks in olive oil at 145 °F for 40 minutes.
  5. Sauté the mushrooms in butter until golden and season.
  6. Warm the cheeks in the glaze, spooning it over until lacquered.
A swoosh of smoked onion purée, two lacquered cheeks, a cluster of mushrooms, the confit yolk set beside them, pickled pepper ribbons, toasted crumbs and savory leaves. Glaze spooned around at the table.
