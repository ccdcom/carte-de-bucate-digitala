🇨🇿 Czechia

Kulajda

Creamy Mushroom Soup
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Kulajda comes from South Bohemia, a land of forests, ponds and mushroom pickers. It is a creamy soup of potatoes and wild mushrooms, soured with vinegar, scented with dill and caraway, and finished with a soft poached egg.

Every Czech family has a mushroom spot it keeps secret, and late summer means baskets of porcini and chanterelles. Out of season, dried mushrooms make an excellent kulajda too.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

South Bohemian Forest Soup

Serves 4 · 45 minutes
Potatoes, mushrooms, sour cream and dill, with a soft-poached egg in each bowl.

INGREDIENTS

  • 6 cups water or light vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds and 1 bay leaf
  • 1 lb potatoes, in small cubes
  • 8 oz fresh wild mushrooms (or 1 oz dried, soaked)
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 2–3 tbsp white vinegar, a pinch of sugar, salt and pepper
  • 4 eggs
  • A large handful of chopped fresh dill

METHOD

  1. Bring the water with the caraway, bay leaf and salt to a boil. Add the potatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
  2. Slice the mushrooms and sauté them in butter for 5 minutes. Add them to the soup (with the soaking water, if using dried).
  3. Whisk the sour cream with the flour and a ladle of hot soup until smooth, then stir it back into the pot. Simmer 5 minutes until creamy.
  4. Season with vinegar, sugar, salt and pepper until gently sour.
  5. Poach the eggs in simmering water with a splash of vinegar for 3 minutes, until the whites are set and the yolks runny.
  6. Stir plenty of dill into the soup and serve with a poached egg in each bowl.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Kulajda, Forest and Egg

Serves 4 · 2 hours
A velvety dill and sour cream velouté, roasted porcini, crisp potato, smoked egg yolk and pine oil.

INGREDIENTS

  • Velouté: 1 cup sour cream, 2 cups mushroom stock (from porcini trimmings), ½ lb potatoes, 2 tbsp butter, cider vinegar
  • 8 oz small porcini or chanterelles, halved and roasted in butter
  • 4 egg yolks, cured lightly in salt and sugar for 1 hour, then cold-smoked briefly
  • Potato: 1 potato, cut into tiny cubes and fried crisp
  • Dill oil and pine oil (young spruce or pine needles blended with oil, strained)
  • Dill fronds, caraway crumble (toasted rye crumbs with caraway)

TECHNIQUE

  1. Cook the potatoes in the mushroom stock, blend with the sour cream and butter into a velvety velouté. Season with cider vinegar and salt.
  2. Roast the mushrooms in foaming butter until golden.
  3. Rinse the cured yolks and smoke them for 5 minutes, or keep them plain and warm them gently.
  4. Fry the potato cubes and make the caraway crumble.
In a warm bowl: roasted mushrooms, the yolk resting in the center, crisp potato cubes, caraway crumble and dill. At the table, pour the velouté around and finish with drops of dill and pine oil.
