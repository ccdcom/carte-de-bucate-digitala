🇵🇹 Portugal

Bacalhau à Brás

Lisbon Cod Dish
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

The Portuguese say there are 365 ways to cook bacalhau, salt cod, one for every day of the year. Bacalhau à Brás is among the most loved: shredded cod, fine matchstick potatoes and onions, bound with softly scrambled egg and finished with black olives and parsley.

It is credited to a tavern keeper named Brás (or Braz) in Lisbon’s Bairro Alto. The “à Brás” technique has since been applied to everything from mushrooms to vegetables, but the cod version remains the heart of Lisbon home cooking.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

The Bairro Alto Skillet

Serves 4 · 45 minutes, plus 24–48 hours soaking
Salt cod, crisp potato straws and creamy eggs, ready in half an hour once the cod is soaked.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 lb salt cod, soaked in cold water for 24–48 hours (change the water 4–5 times)
  • 1 lb potatoes, cut into fine matchsticks (or a bag of good shoestring potato chips)
  • Oil for frying
  • ⅓ cup olive oil
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 6 eggs, beaten with a pinch of pepper
  • A handful of chopped parsley and black olives

METHOD

  1. Poach the soaked cod in simmering water for 5 minutes. Drain, remove skin and bones and shred into fine flakes.
  2. Rinse the potato matchsticks, dry them very well and fry in hot oil (350 °F) in batches until golden and crisp. Drain and salt lightly.
  3. In a large skillet, cook the onions, garlic and bay leaf in the olive oil over medium-low heat for 12–15 minutes, until soft and golden.
  4. Add the cod and stir for 3–4 minutes so it absorbs the oil. Remove the bay leaf.
  5. Fold in the potatoes, then pour in the eggs and stir gently over low heat for about 1 minute, just until creamy and barely set. Taste for salt.
  6. Off the heat, stir in most of the parsley. Serve topped with olives and the rest of the parsley.
Tip. Take the pan off the heat while the eggs still look slightly wet; they will finish cooking on the way to the table.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Brás, Confit and Cream

Serves 4 · 2 hours, plus soaking
Confit cod loin, a silky à Brás of slow-cooked onion and egg, potato threads and black olive, with a cod-skin crisp.

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 thick salt cod loin pieces (5 oz each), desalted for 48 hours, plus the skin
  • 2 cups olive oil, 2 garlic cloves, 1 bay leaf, for the confit
  • 2 onions, finely sliced and cooked slowly for 1 hour until jammy
  • 4 eggs and 2 egg yolks, beaten
  • 1 large potato, cut into ultra-fine threads with a spiralizer
  • Black olive powder: ½ cup Kalamata or Portuguese olives, dried at 175 °F for 4 hours and ground
  • Parsley oil, flaky salt

TECHNIQUE

  1. Confit the cod loins in olive oil with garlic and bay at 150 °F for 20–25 minutes, until they flake into large pearly petals.
  2. Scrape the skin, dry it flat and fry in hot oil until it puffs into a crisp.
  3. Fry the potato threads in small nests at 340 °F until golden. Salt.
  4. Shred a quarter of the cod into the warm jammy onions, add the eggs and stir over very low heat into a soft, custardy scramble.
  5. Fold in a few potato threads at the last second.
