🇳🇴 Norway

Fårikål

Mutton in Cabbage
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Fårikål, “mutton in cabbage,” is Norway’s national dish, voted so by listeners of a national radio program in the 1970s. It is almost absurdly simple: lamb or mutton on the bone, cabbage, whole black peppercorns, a little flour and water, layered in a pot and simmered for hours.

It is eaten in early autumn, when the new lamb and fresh cabbage arrive together, and since the 1990s the last Thursday of September has been Fårikål Feast Day. It fills the whole house with its smell, and tastes even better the next day.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

September Fårikål

Serves 6 · 2½ hours
Lamb, cabbage and peppercorns, layered and simmered until tender.

INGREDIENTS

  • 3–4 lb lamb or mutton on the bone (neck, shoulder, breast), in large pieces
  • 1 large white cabbage (about 4 lb), cut into wedges
  • 2 tbsp whole black peppercorns
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp flour
  • About 2 cups water
  • Boiled potatoes and lingonberry jam, to serve

METHOD

  1. In a large heavy pot, start with a layer of lamb pieces, fat side down, then a layer of cabbage wedges.
  2. Sprinkle each layer with salt, peppercorns and a little flour. Repeat, finishing with cabbage.
  3. Pour in the water, which should come about a quarter of the way up.
  4. Bring to a boil, then cover and simmer gently for 2–2½ hours, until the meat falls off the bone. Do not stir; shake the pot occasionally.
  5. Serve straight from the pot with boiled potatoes, lingonberries and the peppery broth spooned over.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Lamb, Cabbage and Pepper

Serves 4 · 5 hours
Slow-cooked lamb neck, lamb loin, charred and braised cabbage, a peppercorn lamb broth and lingonberry.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 lb lamb neck on the bone and 1 lamb loin (about 12 oz)
  • 1 small pointed or savoy cabbage
  • Broth: lamb bones, 1 onion, 2 tbsp black peppercorns, lightly toasted, 1 bay leaf
  • 2 tbsp butter, salt
  • Lingonberries (fresh or lightly cooked with a little sugar)
  • New potatoes, crisped; dill

TECHNIQUE

  1. Make a clear broth from the roasted bones, onion and peppercorns, simmering 3 hours with the lamb neck in it. Remove the neck when tender and pick the meat in large pieces. Strain and reduce the broth slightly.
  2. Cut the cabbage into wedges. Char one cut side hard in a dry pan, then braise in a little broth and butter until tender.
  3. Roast the loin to 135 °F, rest and slice.
  4. Warm the neck meat in a spoon of broth.
In a wide bowl: a charred cabbage wedge, the picked neck meat and pink loin slices, crisp new potatoes, lingonberries and dill. Pour the hot peppery broth at the table.
