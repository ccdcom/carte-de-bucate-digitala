🇵🇹 Portugal

Caldo Verde

Green Soup
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Caldo verde comes from the green Minho region in northern Portugal: a soup of potatoes, olive oil and finely shredded couve galega, a dark kale, with a slice of smoky chouriço floating in each bowl.

Humble and comforting, it is served at weddings, at the June festivals of Santo António and São João, and at midnight after a long party. It was voted one of Portugal’s Seven Wonders of Gastronomy in 2011.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

The Minho Soup

Serves 6 · 45 minutes
Potato broth, ribbons of kale, olive oil and chouriço, the soup of every Portuguese grandmother.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 lb floury potatoes, peeled and cut
  • 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 quarts water
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 8 oz couve galega or lacinato kale, tough stems removed
  • ⅓ cup good olive oil, plus more to serve
  • 6 oz Portuguese chouriço (or Spanish chorizo), sliced
  • Cornbread (broa), to serve

METHOD

  1. Simmer the potatoes, onion and garlic in the salted water for 20 minutes, until very soft.
  2. Meanwhile roll the kale leaves tightly into cigars and slice them as thinly as possible into fine threads.
  3. Purée the soup until smooth and stir in the olive oil.
  4. Bring back to a simmer, add the kale and cook uncovered for 3–5 minutes, until bright green and just tender.
  5. Brown the chouriço slices briefly in a dry pan.
  6. Serve in bowls with a few slices of chouriço, a thread of olive oil and broa on the side.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Caldo Verde, Smoke and Silk

Serves 4 · 1½ hours
A velvety potato and olive oil velouté with charred kale, chouriço jam, crisp kale leaves and cornbread crumbs.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 lb Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled, 1 leek, white part, 3 cups light chicken stock
  • ½ cup mild Portuguese olive oil, plus a peppery one to finish
  • 4 oz young kale: half in very fine threads, half small whole leaves
  • Chouriço jam: 4 oz chouriço, finely chopped, 1 shallot, 2 tbsp sherry vinegar, 2 tbsp honey
  • ½ cup broa (cornbread) crumbs, toasted in olive oil
  • Smoked salt, kale oil (blanched kale blended with oil and strained)

TECHNIQUE

  1. Soften the leek in olive oil, add the potatoes and stock and simmer for 25 minutes. Blend at high speed, adding the olive oil gradually, until silky. Season and pass through a sieve.
  2. Chouriço jam: render the chouriço with the shallot, add the vinegar and honey and cook until thick and glossy.
  3. Brush the whole kale leaves with oil, salt and bake at 300 °F for 12 minutes until crisp.
  4. Char the kale threads for a few seconds in a very hot dry pan, then toss with a drop of oil and smoked salt.
In a warm bowl: a nest of charred kale threads, a spoon of chouriço jam, cornbread crumbs and two crisp kale leaves. At the table, pour the hot velouté around and finish with kale oil and peppery olive oil.
