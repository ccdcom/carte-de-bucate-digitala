🇧🇪 Belgium

Belgian Waffles

Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Belgium has two great waffles. The Brussels waffle is light, crisp and rectangular, with deep pockets, eaten with powdered sugar or whipped cream. The Liège waffle, said to have been created for the Prince-Bishop of Liège in the 18th century, is made from a brioche dough with pearl sugar that caramelizes in the iron.

Sold warm from street stalls in every Belgian town, they are best eaten on the spot, in a paper napkin, on a cold day.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Liège Street Waffles

Makes 10 · 2½ hours (mostly rising)
A buttery yeast dough studded with pearl sugar that caramelizes in the iron.

INGREDIENTS

  • 3¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 2¼ tsp instant yeast
  • ⅔ cup warm milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar and 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ¾ cup soft butter
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 cup Belgian pearl sugar

METHOD

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, milk, eggs, brown sugar, vanilla and salt and knead for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the soft butter in pieces and knead until the dough is smooth and glossy, about 8 minutes.
  3. Cover and let rise for 1½ hours, until doubled.
  4. Gently knead in the pearl sugar and divide into 10 balls. Rest 15 minutes.
  5. Heat a waffle iron (a deep Belgian one if possible) to medium. Cook each ball for 3–4 minutes, until deep golden and caramelized.
  6. Cool for a minute on a rack (the sugar is very hot) and eat warm.
Tip. Clean the iron with a damp cloth between batches if the caramel builds up.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

The Waffle, Rethought

Serves 6 · 2 hours
A crisp Brussels waffle, light as air, with Tahitian vanilla Chantilly, caramelized apple, speculoos crumble and salted butter caramel.

INGREDIENTS

  • Batter: 2 cups flour, 1 tsp instant yeast, 1¾ cups warm milk, ¼ cup melted butter, 3 eggs, separated, pinch of salt, 1 tbsp sugar, ¼ cup sparkling water
  • Chantilly: 1 cup heavy cream, 1 Tahitian vanilla bean, 2 tbsp powdered sugar
  • Apples: 2 apples, in wedges, caramelized in butter and brown sugar
  • Speculoos crumble: 4 speculoos cookies, crushed with 1 tbsp brown butter
  • Salted butter caramel: ½ cup sugar, ¼ cup cream, 2 tbsp salted butter
  • Powdered sugar

TECHNIQUE

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, milk, yolks, butter, salt and sugar. Rest 1 hour.
  2. Just before cooking, whip the whites to soft peaks and fold them in with the sparkling water.
  3. Cook in a very hot rectangular Brussels waffle iron for 4–5 minutes, until deep golden and crisp.
  4. Whip the cream with the vanilla seeds and sugar.
  5. Make the caramel: melt the sugar to amber, add the cream and butter, and stir until smooth.
Half a waffle on its side, dusted with powdered sugar, a large quenelle of vanilla Chantilly, warm caramelized apples, speculoos crumble scattered over and salted caramel in a small jug at the table.
