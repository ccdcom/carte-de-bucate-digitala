🇩🇪 Germany

Black Forest Cake

Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Schwarzwälder Kirschtorte takes its flavor from the Black Forest’s famous cherry spirit, Kirschwasser. Pastry chef Josef Keller claimed to have created it in 1915 at Café Agner in Bad Godesberg; by the 1930s it had spread across Germany, and it remains one of the country’s best-loved cakes.

Chocolate sponge soaked in kirsch, whipped cream, sour cherries and chocolate shavings: the colors are said to recall the traditional costume of Black Forest women, with its red pom-pom hats.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

The Konditorei Classic

Serves 12 · 2½ hours, plus chilling
Kirsch-soaked chocolate sponge, cherries and plenty of whipped cream.

INGREDIENTS

  • Sponge: 6 eggs, 1 cup sugar, 1 cup flour, ⅓ cup cocoa, 1 tsp baking powder, 3 tbsp melted butter
  • Cherries: 1 jar (24 oz) sour cherries (Morello), drained (keep the juice), 2 tbsp cornstarch, 2 tbsp sugar
  • ½ cup Kirschwasser, mixed with 3 tbsp cherry juice
  • 3 cups heavy cream, very cold, 3 tbsp powdered sugar, 1 tsp vanilla, 1 tsp powdered gelatin (optional, to stabilize)
  • Dark chocolate shavings and 12 fresh or cocktail cherries

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 350 °F and line a 9-inch springform pan.
  2. Beat the eggs and sugar for 10 minutes until very thick. Sift in the flour, cocoa and baking powder and fold, then fold in the butter. Bake 30–35 minutes. Cool and cut into 3 layers.
  3. Bring 1 cup cherry juice to a boil with the sugar, thicken with the cornstarch, add the cherries and cool.
  4. Whip the cream with the powdered sugar and vanilla (and the gelatin, bloomed and melted, if using) to firm peaks.
  5. Place the first layer on a plate, brush with kirsch syrup, spread with cream and spoon the cherries over. Add the second layer, brush, spread with cream. Top with the third layer and brush.
  6. Cover the whole cake with cream, press chocolate shavings onto the sides, pipe 12 rosettes on top and crown each with a cherry. Chill for at least 3 hours.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Black Forest, Deconstructed

Serves 6 · 1 day
Chocolate sponge, kirsch cream, Morello cherries poached and as a sorbet, dark chocolate mousse and crisp chocolate bark.

INGREDIENTS

  • Chocolate sponge: baked thin and cut into irregular pieces, soaked with kirsch syrup
  • Kirsch cream: 1 cup heavy cream, 2 tbsp mascarpone, 1 tbsp powdered sugar, 1 tbsp Kirschwasser
  • Dark chocolate mousse: 4 oz dark chocolate (70%), 1 cup cream whipped, 2 yolks, 2 tbsp sugar
  • Cherries: 1 cup Morello cherries poached in red wine and vanilla; cherry sorbet (2 cups cherry purée, ½ cup sugar, lemon)
  • Chocolate bark: 3 oz tempered dark chocolate spread thin on acetate and broken
  • Cocoa nibs, sorrel or shiso leaves

TECHNIQUE

  1. Churn the cherry sorbet.
  2. Make the mousse and chill in a piping bag.
  3. Whip the kirsch cream until it just holds.
  4. Poach the cherries gently and reduce the liquid to a syrup.
  5. Make the chocolate bark.
On a shallow plate: pieces of kirsch-soaked sponge, piped domes of chocolate mousse and kirsch cream, glossy poached cherries in their syrup, a quenelle of cherry sorbet, shards of chocolate bark standing like trees in a forest, cocoa nibs and a few small leaves.
