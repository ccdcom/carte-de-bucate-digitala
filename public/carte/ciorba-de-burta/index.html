🇷🇴 Romania

Ciorbă de Burtă

Tripe Soup
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Tripe soup came to the Romanian lands with the Ottomans, cousin of the Turkish işkembe çorbası, but Romania made it its own: soured with vinegar, enriched with sour cream and egg yolk, and sharpened at the table with crushed garlic and hot pepper.

Once the soup of late nights and early mornings, eaten in the small hours to restore the weary, it became a national ritual and a test for any Romanian restaurant. Silky, sour and garlicky, it is never served without a fresh hot pepper on the side.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

The Bucharest Bistro Bowl

Serves 6 · 4 hours
Patient, honest cooking: well-cleaned tripe simmered for hours and finished with sour cream and garlic.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 lb cleaned beef tripe (honeycomb if possible)
  • 1 lb beef shank or marrow bones
  • 2 carrots, 1 parsnip, 1 celery root piece, 1 onion
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 6 garlic cloves, crushed with a little salt
  • 4–6 tbsp white wine vinegar, to taste
  • Salt
  • To serve: hot peppers, extra sour cream and garlic sauce (mujdei)

METHOD

  1. Rinse the tripe well, cover with cold water, bring to a boil for 5 minutes and drain. Repeat once.
  2. Put the tripe and bones in a large pot with about 4 quarts of water and a little salt. Simmer gently for 3 hours, skimming often, until the tripe is very tender.
  3. Add the whole vegetables for the last hour.
  4. Lift out the tripe and cut it into thin strips about 2 inches long. Strain the broth and return the tripe to it. Grate or finely slice the carrot and add it too.
  5. In a bowl, whisk the sour cream with the yolks. Whisk in a few ladles of hot broth, then pour the mixture back into the pot, stirring. Heat gently without boiling.
  6. Season with vinegar, salt and the crushed garlic until the soup is pleasantly sour and garlicky.
  7. Serve very hot, with a hot pepper, sour cream and garlic on the side.
Tip. Never let the soup boil after adding the yolks, or it will curdle.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Tripe Consommé, Garlic and Cream

Serves 4 · 2 days
A crystal-clear tripe and bone consommé poured at the table over glazed tripe, a sour-cream and yolk royale and black garlic.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1½ lb honeycomb tripe, thoroughly cleaned
  • 2 lb veal bones and 1 calf’s foot
  • 1 carrot, 1 parsnip, 1 piece of celery root, 1 onion, 1 bay leaf
  • Clarification: 2 egg whites, 3 oz lean ground beef, a few carrot and leek trimmings
  • Royale: ½ cup sour cream, 2 egg yolks, ⅓ cup of the broth, salt
  • 2 tbsp aged white wine vinegar (or cabernet vinegar)
  • 4 cloves black garlic and 1 clove fresh garlic
  • To finish: tiny croutons fried in butter, lovage leaves, chili oil

TECHNIQUE

  1. Blanch the tripe twice. Simmer it with the veal bones, calf’s foot and vegetables in water for 5 hours. Lift out the tripe and cut it into precise 2-inch ribbons. Strain the broth and chill overnight; lift off the fat.
  2. Clarify: whisk the whites with the ground beef and trimmings, stir into the cold broth and bring very slowly to a simmer. Do not stir once the raft forms. Simmer 40 minutes, then ladle through a cloth. Season with vinegar and salt.
  3. Royale: whisk the sour cream, yolks and warm broth, strain, pour into 4 small bowls and steam at 185 °F (or in a 250 °F water bath) for about 20 minutes, until just set.
  4. Warm the tripe ribbons in a little reduced broth with a knob of butter until glazed.
  5. Crush the black garlic with the fresh garlic into a smooth paste.
Place a nest of glazed tripe on the set royale, a dot of black garlic paste, a few croutons and lovage leaves. At the table, pour the hot clear consommé around and finish with three drops of chili oil.
