🇵🇹 Portugal

Caldeirada

Fish and Seafood Stew
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Caldeirada is the fisherman’s stew of the Portuguese coast, named after the caldeira, the cauldron in which fishermen cooked the part of the catch they could not sell. Layers of several fish, potatoes, onions, tomatoes and peppers are stacked in the pot and cooked gently with olive oil, white wine and herbs.

Every fishing town has its version, from Nazaré to Aveiro and the Algarve. The more kinds of fish, the better, and it is never stirred, only shaken.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

The Nazaré Fisherman’s Pot

Serves 6 · 1 hour
Layered, not stirred: fish, potatoes and vegetables cooked together in their own juices.

INGREDIENTS

  • 3 lb mixed firm fish (monkfish, hake, cod, sea bass, conger), in thick pieces, plus 1 lb clams or mussels
  • 2 lb potatoes, in ⅓-inch slices
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 3 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 red and 1 green bell pepper, in strips
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • ½ cup olive oil
  • 1 cup dry white wine
  • 2 bay leaves, 1 tsp sweet paprika, a pinch of chili flakes, salt
  • A large bunch of parsley and cilantro, chopped

METHOD

  1. Season the fish with salt 20 minutes ahead.
  2. In a wide heavy pot, drizzle some oil and lay a layer of onions, then potatoes, tomatoes, peppers, garlic and fish. Sprinkle with paprika, chili, herbs and salt.
  3. Repeat the layers, finishing with tomatoes and herbs, and tuck in the bay leaves.
  4. Pour over the rest of the oil and the wine. Cover and cook over medium-low heat for 30–35 minutes, without stirring; shake the pot from time to time.
  5. Add the clams for the last 8 minutes, until they open.
  6. Serve in deep plates with the broth and plenty of bread.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Caldeirada, Sea and Saffron

Serves 4 · 3 hours
Three fish cooked separately to their exact point, a saffron and shellfish broth, confit potato and pepper, and sea herbs.

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 pieces each (2 oz) of monkfish, red mullet fillet and turbot or hake
  • 12 clams and 8 mussels
  • Broth: 2 lb fish bones and shellfish shells, 1 fennel bulb, 1 onion, 2 tomatoes, 1 cup white wine, saffron, 2 quarts water
  • 8 small potatoes, confit in olive oil at 200 °F for 1 hour
  • 1 red pepper, roasted, peeled and cut into petals
  • Sea herbs (samphire, sea purslane), coriander cress
  • Garlic oil and Piri-Piri oil

TECHNIQUE

  1. Broth: roast the bones and shells, add the vegetables and wine, cover with water and simmer 40 minutes. Strain, add saffron and reduce to 3 cups. Season.
  2. Steam the clams and mussels open in a splash of broth; add their juices to the broth.
  3. Cook the monkfish in foaming butter, the red mullet skin-side down until crisp, and steam the turbot just until opaque.
  4. Warm the confit potatoes and pepper petals in the broth.
  5. Blanch the sea herbs for 10 seconds.
In a wide bowl, arrange the three fish, the shellfish, potatoes and pepper petals, scatter sea herbs and cress. Pour the hot saffron broth at the table and finish with drops of garlic and Piri-Piri oil.
