🇸🇪 Sweden

Prinsesstårta

Princess Cake
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

The Princess Cake is credited to Jenny Åkerström, who taught cooking to the three daughters of Prince Carl of Sweden in the early 20th century. The princesses loved her green cake so much that it took their name.

Sponge, raspberry jam, vanilla custard and a dome of whipped cream, all covered in green marzipan and crowned with a pink marzipan rose: it is Sweden’s celebration cake, and every September Swedish bakeries celebrate Princess Cake Week.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

The Swedish Birthday Cake

Serves 12 · 3 hours, plus chilling
Sponge, jam, custard and a high dome of cream under a green marzipan blanket.

INGREDIENTS

  • Sponge: 4 eggs, ¾ cup sugar, ½ cup flour, ½ cup potato starch, 1 tsp baking powder
  • Custard: 1 cup milk, 3 yolks, 3 tbsp sugar, 2 tbsp cornstarch, 1 tsp vanilla, 1 tbsp butter
  • ½ cup raspberry jam
  • 2 cups heavy cream, whipped with 1 tbsp sugar
  • 10 oz green marzipan (or white marzipan tinted green), a little pink marzipan for the rose
  • Powdered sugar

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 350 °F. Beat the eggs and sugar until very thick, fold in the sifted dry ingredients and bake in a buttered 9-inch pan for 35–40 minutes. Cool and cut into 3 layers.
  2. Custard: heat the milk, whisk the yolks, sugar and cornstarch, combine and cook until thick. Add vanilla and butter. Cool completely.
  3. Place the first layer on a plate, spread with jam and half the custard. Add the second layer and the rest of the custard (fold a little whipped cream into it if you like).
  4. Add the third layer, then heap the remaining whipped cream on top and shape it into a smooth dome with a spatula.
  5. Roll the marzipan thin on powdered sugar into a 14-inch circle. Drape over the cake and smooth the sides; trim the edge.
  6. Make a small pink rose and place it on top. Dust with powdered sugar and chill for at least 2 hours.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Princess, in Ivory

Makes 8 small cakes · 1 day
A small dome of almond sponge, raspberry, vanilla bavarois and cream, wrapped in ivory marzipan with a touch of gold.

INGREDIENTS

  • Almond sponge: 3 eggs, ⅓ cup sugar, ⅓ cup almond flour, ¼ cup flour
  • Raspberry insert: 1 cup raspberry purée, 2 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp gelatin, a few fresh raspberries
  • Vanilla bavarois: 1 cup milk, 1 Tahitian vanilla bean, 3 yolks, ¼ cup sugar, 1½ tsp gelatin, 1 cup cream, whipped
  • Ivory (natural) marzipan, rolled very thin
  • Raspberry sorbet, gold leaf, freeze-dried raspberry powder

TECHNIQUE

  1. Freeze the raspberry insert in small half-sphere molds.
  2. Make the bavarois: vanilla custard to 180 °F, add the bloomed gelatin, cool, then fold in the whipped cream.
  3. In dome molds: bavarois, frozen raspberry insert, more bavarois and a disc of almond sponge. Freeze overnight.
  4. Unmold, thaw slightly and wrap each dome in thin ivory marzipan, smoothing it with your palms.
