🇭🇺 Hungary

Veal Paprikash

Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Paprika became the defining spice of Hungary in the 18th and early 19th centuries, and paprikash, meat braised with onions and paprika and finished with sour cream, became one of its great dishes. Chicken paprikash is the rustic version; veal is its refined cousin from Budapest’s bourgeois kitchens.

It is always served with nokedli, small soft egg dumplings that soak up the velvety red sauce.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Budapest Home Paprikash

Serves 4 · 1¾ hours
Veal braised with onions and paprika, finished with sour cream and served with nokedli.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 lb veal shoulder, in 1½-inch cubes
  • 3 tbsp lard or oil
  • 2 large onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp sweet Hungarian paprika
  • 1 green Hungarian pepper (or ½ green bell pepper) and 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 cup stock or water
  • 1 cup sour cream and 1 tbsp flour
  • Salt
  • Nokedli: 2 cups flour, 2 eggs, ½ cup water, salt, 2 tbsp butter

METHOD

  1. Cook the onions slowly in the lard for 12–15 minutes until soft and golden.
  2. Take the pot off the heat, stir in the paprika (it burns easily), then add the veal, pepper, tomato, salt and stock.
  3. Cover and simmer gently for 1–1¼ hours, until the meat is tender.
  4. Whisk the sour cream with the flour and a ladle of hot sauce, then stir it back into the pot. Simmer gently for 3 minutes, without boiling.
  5. Nokedli: mix the flour, eggs, water and salt into a soft, sticky batter. Press through a nokedli maker or scrape small pieces off a board into boiling salted water. Cook 2 minutes after they float. Drain and toss with butter.
  6. Serve the paprikash with the nokedli and a spoon of extra sour cream.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Veal Cheek, Paprika and Cream

Serves 4 · 4 hours
Slow-braised veal cheek in a glossy paprika sauce, sour cream foam, buttered nokedli and roasted pepper.

INGREDIENTS

  • 8 veal cheeks, trimmed
  • 2 onions, 2 tbsp lard, 2 tbsp sweet paprika, 1 tsp hot paprika, 1 tomato, 2 cups veal stock
  • Sour cream foam: ½ cup sour cream, ¼ cup milk, salt (in a siphon or frothed)
  • Nokedli (as in the classic), browned lightly in butter
  • 1 red pepper, charred and peeled, in strips; 1 Hungarian wax pepper, pickled
  • Paprika oil, dill or chive tips

TECHNIQUE

  1. Sweat the onions in lard until golden, add the paprikas off the heat, then the tomato and stock. Add the cheeks, cover and braise at 300 °F for 3 hours.
  2. Lift out the cheeks. Blend and strain the sauce, then reduce to a glossy coating consistency.
  3. Make the sour cream foam.
  4. Make the nokedli and brown them lightly in butter.
  5. Warm the cheeks in the sauce, basting until glazed.
