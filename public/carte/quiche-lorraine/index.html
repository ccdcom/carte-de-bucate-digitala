Quiche Lorraine

Bacon Custard Tart
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Quiche comes from Lorraine, in northeastern France, near the German border; the name probably comes from the German Kuchen, cake. The earliest versions, from the 16th century, were open bread-dough tarts filled with eggs, cream and smoked bacon.

The true Quiche Lorraine has no cheese and no onion, just lardons and a rich custard in a crisp pastry shell. Gruyère crept in later, and the world adopted the quiche as the classic bistro lunch.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

The Lorraine Farmhouse Tart

Serves 6–8 · 2 hours
Shortcrust, smoky bacon and a deep, wobbling custard.

INGREDIENTS

  • Pastry: 1⅔ cups all-purpose flour, ½ cup cold butter, cubed, 1 egg yolk, 3–4 tbsp ice water, ½ tsp salt
  • 8 oz smoked slab bacon, cut into lardons
  • 4 eggs
  • 1½ cups heavy cream (or crème fraîche)
  • ½ cup whole milk
  • Salt, pepper and a little grated nutmeg
  • ¾ cup grated Gruyère (optional, not traditional)

METHOD

  1. Rub the butter into the flour and salt, add the yolk and enough water to form a dough. Chill 30 minutes.
  2. Roll out and line a 9-inch deep tart pan. Chill 20 minutes.
  3. Blind bake at 375 °F with parchment and beans for 15 minutes, remove the beans and bake 8 minutes more. Lower the oven to 340 °F.
  4. Brown the lardons in a dry pan until golden; drain.
  5. Whisk the eggs, cream and milk with pepper, nutmeg and a little salt.
  6. Scatter the lardons (and cheese, if using) in the shell and pour in the custard.
  7. Bake for 30–35 minutes, until just set with a slight wobble in the center. Rest 15 minutes before cutting.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Lorraine, Smoke and Custard

Serves 6 · 4 hours
A thin, crisp pastry case, smoked bacon custard set as smooth as silk, a bacon consommé and Comté crisp.

INGREDIENTS

  • Pâte brisée: 1¼ cups flour, ½ cup butter, 1 yolk, ice water, salt
  • Smoked cream: 1½ cups heavy cream infused with 4 oz smoked bacon rind for 1 hour
  • Custard: the infused cream, 3 eggs + 2 yolks, ½ cup milk, nutmeg, white pepper
  • Lardons: 4 oz smoked pork belly, cooked slowly then crisped
  • Bacon consommé: 1 cup clear chicken stock simmered with bacon trimmings, strained
  • Comté crisp: ½ cup grated aged Comté
  • Chives, pea shoots

TECHNIQUE

  1. Line six 3-inch tart rings with very thin pastry, chill and blind bake at 350 °F until golden. Brush the inside with egg yolk and bake 1 more minute to seal.
  2. Infuse the cream with the bacon rind, strain, then blend with the eggs, yolks, milk and seasoning. Rest so the bubbles disappear.
  3. Place a few lardons in each case and fill with custard to the rim.
  4. Bake at 250 °F for 20–25 minutes, until just set and perfectly smooth.
  5. Bake small mounds of Comté on a silicone mat at 350 °F until lacy and golden.
