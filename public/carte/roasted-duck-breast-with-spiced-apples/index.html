🇩🇰 Denmark

Roasted Duck Breast with Spiced Apples

Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Roast duck with apples and prunes is the centerpiece of the Danish Christmas Eve dinner (juleaften), served with caramelized potatoes and red cabbage after a candlelit dance around the Christmas tree.

The modern version takes just the breast, roasted until the skin is crisp and golden, glazed with juniper and honey, and served with gently spiced apples and a light game jus.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Danish Christmas Duck

Serves 4–6 · 3½ hours
Whole duck stuffed with apples and prunes, with caramelized potatoes and red cabbage.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 whole duck (about 5–6 lb), salt and pepper
  • 2 tart apples, quartered, 12 pitted prunes, a few sprigs of thyme
  • Caramelized potatoes: 2 lb small potatoes, boiled and peeled, ½ cup sugar, 3 tbsp butter
  • Red cabbage: 1 head, shredded, 3 tbsp butter, ½ cup red currant juice or jelly, ¼ cup vinegar, 3 tbsp sugar
  • Gravy: the pan juices, 1 tbsp flour, 1 cup stock, a splash of cream

METHOD

  1. Season the duck inside and out. Fill with apples, prunes and thyme.
  2. Roast on a rack at 325 °F for about 2½ hours, pouring off the fat every half hour. Turn up to 425 °F for the last 15 minutes to crisp the skin.
  3. Red cabbage: cook the cabbage in butter for 10 minutes, add the juice, vinegar and sugar and simmer, covered, for 1 hour.
  4. Caramelized potatoes: melt the sugar in a pan to golden caramel, add the butter, then the potatoes, and turn them until glossy and golden.
  5. Make a gravy from the skimmed pan juices, flour, stock and cream.
  6. Carve the duck and serve with the apples and prunes, potatoes, cabbage and gravy.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Duck, Juniper and Apple

Serves 4 · 2 hours
Juniper and honey glazed duck breast, apples spiced with cinnamon and clove, celeriac purée and a light game jus.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 large duck breasts (Magret), skin scored
  • Glaze: 2 tbsp honey, 6 juniper berries, crushed, 1 tsp sherry vinegar
  • Apples: 2 crisp apples, in wedges, 2 tbsp butter, 1 tbsp brown sugar, a pinch of cinnamon and clove, a splash of Calvados
  • Celeriac purée: ½ celeriac, cooked in milk, blended with butter
  • Game jus: 1 cup duck or game stock, ½ cup red wine, 1 shallot, thyme, 1 tbsp cold butter
  • Pickled red cabbage, thyme, flaky salt

TECHNIQUE

  1. Reduce the wine with the shallot and thyme, add the stock and reduce to a light jus. Finish with butter.
  2. Make the celeriac purée.
  3. Sauté the apples in butter with the sugar and spices until golden but holding their shape; flambé with Calvados.
  4. Start the duck skin side down in a cold pan and render for 8–10 minutes until crisp. Turn and sear briefly, brush with glaze and roast at 400 °F for 5–6 minutes to 130 °F internal. Rest 8 minutes.
Thick slices of glazed duck, pink and glossy, on a swoosh of celeriac purée, spiced apple wedges alongside, a little pickled red cabbage, thyme and game jus spooned at the table.
