Torta Garash

Chocolate–Walnut Cake
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Garash cake was created in Ruse in 1885 by Kosta Garash, a Hungarian confectioner who settled on the Danube and ran the city’s finest pastry shop. Rich and flourless, it is made only of walnuts, egg whites, sugar and chocolate.

Thin, chewy walnut meringue layers are stacked with dark chocolate cream and covered in a shiny chocolate glaze. For Bulgarians it is the cake of celebrations and the pride of the Danube city.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

The Ruse Pastry-Shop Cake

Serves 12 · 2½ hours, plus overnight chilling
Five walnut-meringue layers and a rich chocolate cream, no flour at all.

INGREDIENTS

  • Layers: 10 egg whites, 1¼ cups sugar, 2½ cups walnuts, finely ground, 1 tbsp flour or cornstarch (optional, for easier handling)
  • Cream: 10 oz dark chocolate, 1¼ cups heavy cream, 2 tbsp butter
  • Walnut halves to decorate

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 325 °F. Draw five 9-inch circles on sheets of parchment.
  2. Beat the whites to soft peaks, then add the sugar gradually and beat to stiff, glossy peaks. Fold in the ground walnuts (and flour, if using).
  3. Spread the mixture evenly into the five circles.
  4. Bake each layer for 12–15 minutes, until golden and dry to the touch. Peel off the paper while still warm and let cool.
  5. Bring the cream to a simmer, pour over the chopped chocolate, stir until smooth and add the butter. Let it cool until spreadable.
  6. Stack the layers, spreading cream between each one, then cover the top and sides. Decorate with walnut halves and chill overnight.
Tip. The layers are fragile: slide a large flat plate or cake board under each one when stacking.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Garash, Danube and Smoke

Serves 8 · 1 day
A slim bar of walnut dacquoise and smoked dark-chocolate ganache, walnut praline, crème fraîche sorbet and cocoa tuile.

INGREDIENTS

  • Dacquoise: 5 egg whites, ⅔ cup sugar, 1¼ cups toasted walnuts, finely ground
  • Ganache: 7 oz dark chocolate (70%), 1 cup heavy cream, lightly smoked over applewood, 1 tbsp honey, 2 tbsp butter
  • Praline: 1 cup toasted walnuts, ½ cup sugar, pinch of sea salt
  • Sorbet: 1 cup crème fraîche, ½ cup water, ½ cup sugar, juice of ½ lemon
  • Glaze: 4 oz dark chocolate, ⅓ cup cream, 1 tbsp glucose
  • Cocoa nibs and gold leaf (optional)

TECHNIQUE

  1. Make the dacquoise as for the classic layers, spread into a ⅛-inch sheet and bake at 325 °F for 14 minutes. Cut into 4 equal rectangles.
  2. Heat the smoked cream with the honey, pour over the chocolate, blend and add the butter.
  3. Layer dacquoise and ganache in a rectangular frame, finishing with dacquoise. Freeze for 3 hours, then cut into slim bars.
  4. Praline: caramelize the sugar, stir in the walnuts and salt, cool and blend into a smooth paste.
  5. Sorbet: dissolve the sugar in the water, cool, whisk into the crème fraîche with the lemon juice and churn.
  6. Glaze the bars with the warm glaze and set in the fridge.
