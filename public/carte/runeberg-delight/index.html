Runeberg Delight

Runeberg Torte
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Fredrika Runeberg, a writer herself and wife of Finland’s national poet Johan Ludvig Runeberg, is said to have created this little tart from what was in the pantry: almonds, breadcrumbs, a splash of rum and a spoon of raspberry jam. The poet is said to have eaten one every morning.

Runeberg tortes are eaten every year on 5 February, Runeberg’s birthday, recognizable by their cylinder shape, raspberry jam center and ring of white icing. In Helsinki, chefs make them lighter, with a bright raspberry jewel and a precise sugar circle.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Runebergintorttu

Makes 10 · 1 hour
Moist almond and rum cakes, crowned with raspberry jam and a ring of icing.

INGREDIENTS

  • ½ cup soft butter
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • ¾ cup ground almonds
  • ½ cup gingerbread or plain breadcrumbs, plus ½ cup flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder, ½ tsp cardamom
  • ⅓ cup heavy cream
  • Syrup: ¼ cup water, 2 tbsp sugar, 2 tbsp rum (or orange juice)
  • Raspberry jam
  • Icing: ½ cup powdered sugar with 1–2 tsp water

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 400 °F. Butter 10 cups of a muffin pan (or cylindrical molds).
  2. Beat the butter and sugar until fluffy, add the egg, then fold in the almonds, crumbs, flour, baking powder, cardamom and cream.
  3. Fill the cups two-thirds full and bake for 15–18 minutes, until golden.
  4. Boil the water and sugar, add the rum and brush or drizzle over the warm cakes.
  5. Unmold and cool. Make a small hollow on top of each, fill with raspberry jam and pipe a ring of icing around it.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Runeberg, Raspberry and Rum

Serves 6 · 4 hours
A light almond and rum sponge, a raspberry ‘jewel’, a precise sugar ring, sour cream ice cream and freeze-dried raspberry.

INGREDIENTS

  • Sponge: the classic batter made lighter with 1 extra egg white whipped and folded in, baked in small rings
  • Rum syrup with a strip of orange peel
  • Raspberry jewel: 1 cup raspberry purée, 2 tbsp sugar, ½ tsp agar-agar, set in small hemisphere molds
  • Royal icing: 1 egg white, 1¼ cups powdered sugar, a few drops of lemon
  • Sour cream ice cream: 1 cup sour cream, ½ cup milk, ⅓ cup sugar, lemon
  • Fresh raspberries, freeze-dried raspberry, toasted almond slivers

TECHNIQUE

  1. Churn the sour cream ice cream.
  2. Bake the sponges, soak them with the rum syrup and trim into neat cylinders.
  3. Set the raspberry gel in hemisphere molds and unmold.
  4. Pipe a perfect fine ring of royal icing on top of each cylinder and place the raspberry jewel in the center.
The small torte off-center with its raspberry jewel and white ring, a quenelle of sour cream ice cream on a few almond slivers, halved fresh raspberries and a dusting of freeze-dried raspberry.
