Baklava

Sweet Phyllo Pastry with Nuts
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Layered pastries of nuts and honey go back to Byzantine and Ottoman kitchens, and the palace of Topkapı made baklava a dish of sultans. Greece, Turkey and the whole Balkan world share it, each with its own nuts, spices and syrup.

The Greek version usually uses walnuts with cinnamon and cloves, and a honey syrup scented with lemon. Tradition says a festive baklava should have 33 sheets of phyllo, one for each year of Christ’s life.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Grandmother’s Walnut Baklava

Makes about 30 pieces · 2 hours, plus overnight resting
Walnuts, cinnamon, butter and honey syrup, cut into diamonds before baking.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 lb phyllo dough, thawed
  • 1 cup butter, melted
  • 4 cups walnuts, chopped
  • 2 tbsp sugar, 2 tsp cinnamon, ¼ tsp ground cloves
  • Syrup: 1½ cups sugar, 1 cup water, ½ cup honey, 1 strip lemon peel, 1 cinnamon stick, juice of ½ lemon

METHOD

  1. Make the syrup first: boil the sugar, water, lemon peel and cinnamon for 10 minutes, add the honey and lemon juice and let it cool completely.
  2. Heat the oven to 325 °F. Mix the walnuts with the sugar and spices.
  3. Butter a 9 × 13-inch pan. Lay in 8 sheets of phyllo, brushing each with butter.
  4. Scatter a third of the nuts, then 4 buttered sheets; repeat twice more. Finish with 8 buttered sheets on top.
  5. With a sharp knife, cut all the way through into diamonds.
  6. Bake for about 1 hour, until deep golden and crisp.
  7. Pour the cold syrup over the hot baklava slowly, letting it soak in. Leave uncovered at room temperature overnight before serving.
Tip. Hot pastry and cold syrup (or the reverse) keep the layers crisp. Never pour hot syrup on hot baklava.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Baklava, Thyme Honey and Pistachio

Serves 6 · 1 day
A crisp stacked baklava of three nuts, thyme-honey syrup, Greek yogurt ice cream and a mastiha cream.

INGREDIENTS

  • 12 sheets phyllo and ½ cup clarified butter
  • Filling: ½ cup each walnuts, pistachios and blanched almonds, toasted and finely chopped, 2 tbsp sugar, ½ tsp cinnamon, pinch of sea salt
  • Syrup: ⅔ cup Greek thyme honey, ⅓ cup water, 2 tbsp sugar, strip of orange peel, 1 sprig thyme
  • Yogurt ice cream: 2 cups Greek yogurt, ½ cup sugar, ¼ cup whole milk, 2 tbsp honey
  • Mastiha cream: ½ cup heavy cream, ¼ tsp ground mastiha (mastic), 1 tbsp powdered sugar
  • Pistachio dust, candied orange zest, edible flowers

TECHNIQUE

  1. Ice cream: blend the yogurt, sugar, milk and honey, chill and churn.
  2. Syrup: bring all ingredients to a simmer, infuse 20 minutes, strain and cool.
  3. Stack 4 phyllo sheets brushed with butter, scatter a thin layer of nuts; repeat to make 3 layers of nuts and finish with 4 sheets. Press between two trays.
  4. Bake between the trays at 340 °F for 40 minutes, then remove the top tray and bake 10 minutes more until golden. Cut into neat rectangles while hot and spoon over the cold syrup.
  5. Whip the cream with the mastiha and sugar to soft peaks.
