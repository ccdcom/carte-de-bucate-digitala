Joffre

Chocolate Cake
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

In the 1920s Marshal Joseph Joffre, the hero of the Marne, visited Bucharest at the invitation of King Ferdinand and Queen Marie. At Casa Capșa, master confectioner Grigore Capșa created a chocolate cake for him, shaped like a small cylinder and modest in size, because the marshal was diabetic.

Layers of chocolate sponge and dense chocolate cream under a glossy glaze: the Joffre cake became a symbol of interwar Bucharest elegance and is still on the menu of every Romanian pastry shop.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

The Family Joffre

Serves 10 · 3 hours, plus overnight chilling
The home version: a round chocolate cake with rich chocolate buttercream and a shiny glaze.

INGREDIENTS

  • Sponge: 6 eggs, ¾ cup sugar, ¾ cup all-purpose flour, ¼ cup cocoa powder, 1 tsp baking powder, 3 tbsp oil
  • Syrup: ½ cup water, ¼ cup sugar, 1 tbsp rum
  • Cream: 7 oz dark chocolate (60–70%), 1 cup butter, softened, ¾ cup powdered sugar, 3 tbsp cocoa, 3 egg yolks or 3 tbsp milk
  • Glaze: 5 oz dark chocolate, ½ cup heavy cream, 1 tbsp butter

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 350 °F and line a 9-inch springform pan.
  2. Beat the eggs with the sugar for 8–10 minutes, until thick and pale. Sift in the flour, cocoa and baking powder and fold gently; fold in the oil last.
  3. Bake for 30–35 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean. Cool completely and cut horizontally into 3 layers.
  4. Boil the water and sugar, cool, and add the rum.
  5. Cream: melt the chocolate and cool to lukewarm. Beat the butter with the powdered sugar and cocoa until fluffy, add the yolks (or milk), then the chocolate.
  6. Layer: sponge, syrup, a third of the cream; repeat; cover the top and sides with the remaining cream. Chill for 2 hours.
  7. Glaze: pour the hot cream over the chopped chocolate, stir until smooth, add the butter. Pour over the cold cake and chill overnight.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Joffre, Capșa 1920

Makes 8 · 1 day
Individual domes of cocoa sponge and dark chocolate crémeux under a mirror glaze, the Capșa shape made precise.

INGREDIENTS

  • Sponge: 3 eggs, ⅓ cup sugar, ⅓ cup flour, 2 tbsp cocoa
  • Crémeux: 1 cup heavy cream, ½ cup whole milk, 4 egg yolks, 3 tbsp sugar, 7 oz dark chocolate (66–70%), chopped
  • Chocolate mousse: 5 oz dark chocolate, 1¼ cups heavy cream, whipped to soft peaks, 2 yolks, 2 tbsp sugar
  • Mirror glaze: ½ cup water, ¾ cup sugar, ½ cup cocoa, ½ cup heavy cream, 2 tsp powdered gelatin bloomed in 3 tbsp water
  • Rum syrup and gold leaf (optional), cocoa nibs

TECHNIQUE

  1. Bake the sponge thin on a sheet at 375 °F for 8 minutes. Cut 16 discs that fit your molds (2½-inch dome or cylinder molds).
  2. Crémeux: make a custard with the cream, milk, yolks and sugar to 180 °F, pour over the chocolate and blend. Pour into small inserts and freeze.
  3. Mousse: whisk the yolks and sugar over a water bath until thick, fold into the melted chocolate, then fold in the whipped cream.
  4. Assemble upside down: mousse in the mold, a frozen crémeux insert, more mousse, a sponge disc brushed with rum syrup. Freeze overnight.
  5. Glaze: boil the water, sugar and cream, whisk in the cocoa, add the gelatin and strain. Use at 95 °F over the frozen cakes. Thaw in the fridge 3 hours.
One glossy Joffre on a small round of cocoa sablé, a touch of gold leaf on top, a spoon of cocoa nibs and a quenelle of lightly whipped cream to the side.
