🇵🇱 Poland

Polish Duck in Sour Cherry Sauce

Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Roast duck with fruit is an old dish of Polish manor kitchens, inspired by medieval habits of serving game with sweet and sour sauces. Sour cherries, grown in Poland for centuries, are its natural partner.

The duck is roasted slowly until the skin is crisp and the meat tender, then served with a glossy cherry sauce, potatoes or kopytka dumplings and braised red cabbage. It is a dish for family celebrations and St. Martin’s Day in November.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Manor House Roast Duck

Serves 4 · 3 hours, plus overnight seasoning
Whole duck stuffed with apples, roasted slowly, with a sour cherry sauce.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 whole duck (about 5 lb)
  • 1 tbsp salt, 1 tsp marjoram, ½ tsp black pepper, 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tart apples, quartered
  • Sauce: 2 cups pitted sour cherries (fresh, frozen or jarred), ½ cup cherry juice or red wine, 2–3 tbsp sugar, 1 tbsp cornstarch, 1 tbsp butter, a pinch of cinnamon
  • To serve: boiled potatoes and braised red cabbage

METHOD

  1. Rub the duck inside and out with the salt, marjoram, pepper and garlic. Refrigerate uncovered overnight.
  2. Fill the cavity with the apples. Prick the skin all over without piercing the meat.
  3. Roast on a rack at 325 °F for about 2½ hours, pouring off the fat every 30 minutes (keep it for potatoes). Raise the oven to 425 °F for the last 15 minutes to crisp the skin.
  4. Sauce: simmer the cherries with the juice, sugar and cinnamon for 5 minutes. Thicken with the cornstarch mixed with a little water, then stir in the butter.
  5. Rest the duck 15 minutes, carve, and serve with the cherry sauce, potatoes and red cabbage.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Duck, Cherry and Beet

Serves 4 · 1 day
Pink-roasted duck breast and crisp leg croquette, a sour cherry and red wine jus, beet purée and roasted pear.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 duck breasts and 2 duck legs (legs confit and shredded)
  • Croquette: confit leg meat, shallot, marjoram, breadcrumbs
  • Jus: 1 cup duck stock, ½ cup red wine, ½ cup sour cherries, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tsp red wine vinegar, 1 tbsp cold butter
  • Beet purée: 2 roasted beets, butter, a dash of vinegar
  • 1 pear, cut into wedges and roasted in duck fat
  • Pickled sour cherries, marjoram, kopytka (potato dumplings) or mashed potato

TECHNIQUE

  1. Shape the confit meat into small croquettes, bread them and chill.
  2. Reduce the wine and duck stock, add the cherries, honey and vinegar, reduce to a glossy jus and finish with butter.
  3. Blend the beets with butter until silky.
  4. Score the breasts, start skin side down in a cold pan and render slowly for 8–10 minutes, then sear the flesh for 1 minute and roast at 400 °F to 130 °F internal. Rest 8 minutes.
  5. Fry the croquettes until golden; roast the pear.
Slices of pink duck breast, a crisp croquette, a swoosh of beet purée, roasted pear wedges, pickled cherries, marjoram leaves and the cherry jus spooned around.
