🇦🇹 Austria

Wiener Schnitzel

Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Legend says Field Marshal Radetzky brought the breaded cutlet home from Milan, where cotoletta alla milanese was already famous. Historians doubt it, but by the 19th century the Viennese had made the dish their own.

By Austrian law, a true Wiener Schnitzel must be veal, pounded thin, breaded and fried in plenty of fat until the coating puffs up into golden waves, separate from the meat. It is served with lemon, a potato salad and lingonberries.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

The Viennese Gasthaus Schnitzel

Serves 4 · 45 minutes
Thin veal, a souffléd golden crumb, lemon and lukewarm potato salad.

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 veal cutlets from the top round, about 5 oz each
  • Salt
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten with 1 tbsp cream
  • 1½ cups fine dry breadcrumbs (ideally from day-old rolls)
  • Clarified butter or lard (or oil with some butter), about 2 cups for frying
  • Lemon wedges, parsley, lingonberry jam
  • Potato salad: 1½ lb waxy potatoes, ½ cup warm beef broth, 1 small onion, 3 tbsp vinegar, 3 tbsp oil, mustard, chives

METHOD

  1. Potato salad: boil, peel and slice the potatoes while warm. Pour over the warm broth mixed with finely chopped onion, vinegar, oil and mustard. Season and leave 30 minutes to absorb. Add chives.
  2. Place the veal between plastic wrap and pound evenly to about ⅛ inch thick. Season with salt.
  3. Dip each cutlet in flour, then egg, then breadcrumbs, pressing lightly. Do not press the crumbs too firmly.
  4. Heat at least ¾ inch of fat to 340 °F in a large pan.
  5. Fry one schnitzel at a time for 2–3 minutes per side, swirling the pan so the hot fat washes over the top. The crust should puff into waves.
  6. Drain on paper towels and serve immediately with lemon, parsley, lingonberries and potato salad.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Schnitzel, Butter and Brioche

Serves 4 · 2 hours
Veal loin in a brioche crumb, fried in brown butter, with a potato and cucumber salad, lingonberry gel and capers.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1¼ lb milk-fed veal loin, cut into 4 and butterflied thin
  • Flour, 2 eggs beaten with 2 tbsp cream, 2 cups fresh brioche crumbs, dried slightly
  • 2 cups clarified butter, finished with 2 tbsp brown butter
  • Salad: fingerling potatoes in warm veal broth vinaigrette, cucumber ribbons in sour cream and dill
  • Lingonberry gel: ½ cup lingonberry juice or jam, ¼ tsp agar-agar
  • Fried capers, anchovy (optional), lemon, parsley cress

TECHNIQUE

  1. Bread the veal as for the classic, using the fine brioche crumbs.
  2. Dress the warm potatoes in the broth vinaigrette; dress the cucumber separately.
  3. Make the lingonberry gel: boil with agar, set and blend smooth.
  4. Fry the schnitzel in the clarified butter at 340 °F, swirling, until the crumb puffs and turns deep gold; baste with brown butter at the end.
