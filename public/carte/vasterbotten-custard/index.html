🇸🇪 Sweden

Västerbotten Custard

Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Västerbotten cheese has been made in the village of Burträsk in northern Sweden since the 1870s. Local legend says a dairymaid named Ulrika, distracted by a suitor, stirred the curd too long, accidentally creating a hard, sharp, crumbly cheese with an unusual depth.

Today it is a Swedish treasure, essential at the August crayfish party in a savory pie (Västerbottenpaj). Baked as a custard and served with rye and dill, it shows how simple ingredients become refined through time and care.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Västerbotten Cheese Pie

Serves 8 · 1½ hours
The classic Swedish crayfish-party pie: a buttery crust and a sharp cheese custard.

INGREDIENTS

  • Crust: 1⅓ cups flour, ½ cup cold butter, 2–3 tbsp cold water, pinch of salt
  • 8 oz Västerbotten cheese (or aged Parmesan mixed with sharp aged cheddar), grated
  • 3 eggs
  • 1¼ cups heavy cream (or half cream, half milk)
  • White pepper, a little nutmeg
  • To serve: sour cream, chopped red onion, dill and whitefish roe (löjrom) or salmon roe

METHOD

  1. Rub the butter into the flour and salt, add water to form a dough, press into a 10-inch tart pan and chill 30 minutes.
  2. Blind bake at 400 °F for 10 minutes with parchment and beans, then 5 minutes without. Lower the oven to 350 °F.
  3. Whisk the eggs with the cream, pepper and nutmeg, and stir in the cheese.
  4. Pour into the crust and bake for 25–30 minutes, until golden and just set.
  5. Serve warm or at room temperature with sour cream, red onion, dill and roe.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Västerbotten, Rye and Roe

Serves 6 as a starter · 2 hours
A silky baked Västerbotten custard with rye crumble, pickled cucumber, dill oil and bleak roe.

INGREDIENTS

  • Custard: 4 oz Västerbotten, grated, 1 cup cream, ½ cup milk, 3 egg yolks + 1 egg, white pepper
  • Rye crumble: 2 slices dark rye, toasted with butter, caraway and a pinch of sugar, then crushed
  • Pickled cucumber: 1 cucumber in ribbons, quick-pickled in vinegar, sugar and water
  • 1 oz Kalix löjrom (bleak roe) or another small golden roe
  • Dill oil, dill fronds, crème fraîche

TECHNIQUE

  1. Warm the cream and milk with the cheese until it melts, cool slightly, blend with the eggs and yolks and strain.
  2. Pour into 6 small bowls or glasses and bake in a water bath at 250 °F for 30–35 minutes, until just set. Serve warm.
  3. Make the rye crumble and the pickled cucumber.
