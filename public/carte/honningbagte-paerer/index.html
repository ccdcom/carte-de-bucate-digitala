Honningbagte Pærer

Honey-Baked Pears
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

In old Danish farmhouses, pears were kept through autumn and winter in cool cellars, and baking them with honey was the simplest way to make them tender and fragrant. Cinnamon and clove, brought by Danish ships through the spice trade, slipped naturally into the recipe.

Served warm with a crisp almond crumble or a spoon of cold cream, honey-baked pears prove the Danish point: you do not need elaborate techniques, just good fruit, warm spices and a little honey.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Farmhouse Honey Pears

Serves 4 · 1 hour
Pears baked slowly in honey, butter and spices, with an almond crumble and cream.

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 ripe but firm pears, halved and cored
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 cinnamon stick, 3 cloves, 1 strip of lemon peel
  • ½ cup apple juice or white wine
  • Crumble: ½ cup flour, ¼ cup sliced almonds, 3 tbsp sugar, 3 tbsp cold butter
  • Lightly whipped cream or vanilla ice cream

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 350 °F.
  2. Place the pears cut side up in a baking dish. Drizzle with honey, dot with butter and add the spices, lemon peel and juice.
  3. Bake for 35–45 minutes, basting every 10 minutes, until tender and golden.
  4. Rub the crumble ingredients together, spread on a sheet and bake alongside for 15 minutes, until golden.
  5. Serve the warm pears with their syrup, a scattering of crumble and whipped cream.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Pear, Honey and Brown Butter

Serves 4 · 3 hours
A whole pear baked in heather honey and spices, brown butter crumble, buttermilk ice cream and honey tuile.

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 small pears, peeled, stems left on, cored from below
  • Glaze: ⅓ cup heather or forest honey, 2 tbsp butter, ½ vanilla bean, 1 cinnamon stick, 2 cloves, ½ cup dry cider
  • Brown butter crumble: ½ cup flour, ¼ cup ground almonds, 3 tbsp brown sugar, 3 tbsp brown butter, chilled
  • Buttermilk ice cream: 1½ cups buttermilk, ½ cup cream, ½ cup sugar, lemon zest
  • Honey tuile: 2 tbsp honey, 2 tbsp butter, 2 tbsp sugar, 2 tbsp flour
  • Bee pollen, thyme flowers

TECHNIQUE

  1. Churn the buttermilk ice cream.
  2. Bake the pears standing in a small dish with the glaze ingredients at 325 °F for about 1 hour, basting often, until glossy and tender. Reduce the juices to a syrup.
  3. Bake the crumble until golden and crisp.
  4. Spread the tuile batter thin and bake at 340 °F for 6–7 minutes; shape while warm.
A glossy whole pear standing in the center, a spoon of honey syrup over it, crumble at its base, a quenelle of buttermilk ice cream, a curved honey tuile, a pinch of bee pollen and thyme flowers.
