🇳🇴 Norway

Stekt Torsk med Asparges

Pan-Fried Cod with Asparagus
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Cod has shaped Norway for more than a thousand years: traded by the Vikings, dried as stockfish on the racks of Lofoten, and even pictured on the 200-krone banknote. Coastal families cooked it simply, poached with potatoes, as in mølje, with roe and liver.

With the New Nordic movement, chefs paired winter cod with the first spring asparagus: crisp, pan-fried fish, bright green spears and a light butter sauce, a dish about the passage from cold seas to new life.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Coastal Kitchen Cod

Serves 4 · 35 minutes
Pan-fried cod with asparagus, boiled potatoes and melted butter with dill.

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 thick cod fillets (6 oz each), skin on
  • 1 tsp salt (sprinkled on the fish 20 minutes ahead)
  • 1 lb green asparagus, trimmed
  • 1½ lb small potatoes
  • 2 tbsp oil and 6 tbsp butter
  • Lemon, chopped dill, black pepper

METHOD

  1. Salt the cod and leave for 20 minutes; pat dry.
  2. Boil the potatoes in salted water until tender.
  3. Blanch the asparagus for 2–3 minutes in salted boiling water and drain.
  4. Heat the oil in a skillet and fry the cod skin side down over medium-high heat for 4–5 minutes, until golden. Add 2 tablespoons butter, turn the fish and cook 1–2 minutes more, basting.
  5. Melt the rest of the butter with lemon juice and dill.
  6. Serve the cod with asparagus, potatoes and the dill butter poured over.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Cod, Asparagus and Beurre Blanc

Serves 4 · 1½ hours
Skrei cod with crisp skin, green and white asparagus, a smoked butter sauce, cod roe and pea shoots.

INGREDIENTS

  • 4 portions skrei (winter cod) loin, 5 oz each, lightly salted for 30 minutes
  • 8 green and 8 white asparagus spears
  • Smoked beurre blanc: 1 shallot, ⅓ cup white wine, 2 tbsp white wine vinegar, 2 tbsp cream, ¾ cup cold butter (part of it lightly smoked), lemon
  • Asparagus purée: asparagus trimmings, butter, a little stock
  • 1 oz cod or lumpfish roe, dill, pea shoots, chive oil

TECHNIQUE

  1. Reduce the wine and vinegar with the shallot to 2 tablespoons, add the cream, then whisk in the cold butter piece by piece. Season with lemon, strain and keep warm.
  2. Cook the white asparagus in salted, sugared water for 8 minutes and the green ones for 3. Make a quick purée from the trimmings.
  3. Fry the cod skin side down until crisp, then baste with butter until just opaque, about 125 °F inside, so it flakes into glossy petals.
