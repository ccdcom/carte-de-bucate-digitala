Apple Strudel

The story behind the dish

The strudel came to Vienna through the Habsburg Empire from Hungary and, further back, from the layered pastries of the Ottomans. The oldest known strudel recipe, handwritten, dates from 1696 and is kept in Vienna’s city library.

Its dough is pulled by hand on a floured tablecloth until you can read a newspaper through it. Filled with apples, raisins, cinnamon and buttery breadcrumbs, it has cousins all over Central Europe, from the Hungarian rétes to the Romanian ștrudel.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Grandmother’s Pulled Strudel

Serves 8 · 2½ hours
Hand-stretched dough, apples, raisins and buttered crumbs, served warm with cream or vanilla sauce.

INGREDIENTS

  • Dough: 1⅔ cups bread flour, ½ cup lukewarm water, 3 tbsp oil, ½ tsp salt, 1 tsp vinegar
  • Filling: 3 lb tart apples, peeled and thinly sliced, juice of 1 lemon, ½ cup sugar, 2 tsp cinnamon, ½ cup raisins soaked in rum
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs toasted in 4 tbsp butter
  • ½ cup melted butter for brushing
  • Powdered sugar; whipped cream or vanilla sauce to serve

METHOD

  1. Knead the dough ingredients for 10 minutes until very smooth. Brush with oil, cover and rest in a warm place for 1 hour.
  2. Mix the apples with lemon juice, sugar, cinnamon and raisins.
  3. Cover a table with a floured cloth. Roll the dough out a little, then stretch it with the backs of your hands from the center outwards until it is paper-thin and about 3 × 2 feet. Cut off the thick edges.
  4. Brush with melted butter. Scatter the toasted crumbs over the lower third, then the apples.
  5. Lift the cloth to roll the strudel up tightly, tuck in the ends and roll onto a lined baking sheet, seam down, in a horseshoe if needed.
  6. Brush with butter and bake at 375 °F for 40–45 minutes, brushing again halfway.
  7. Dust with powdered sugar and serve warm with whipped cream or vanilla sauce.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Strudel, Apple and Vanilla

Serves 6 · 4 hours
A crisp, slim strudel of caramelized apple, a Calvados-raisin compote, vanilla custard and sour cream ice cream.

INGREDIENTS

  • Strudel dough (as for the classic) or 6 sheets of thin phyllo
  • Apples: 4 apples, in small dice, caramelized with 3 tbsp butter, ¼ cup brown sugar, cinnamon and vanilla
  • Raisin compote: ½ cup golden raisins, ¼ cup Calvados, 2 tbsp apple juice
  • Brown butter crumbs: ½ cup panko, 2 tbsp brown butter, 1 tbsp sugar
  • Vanilla custard: 1 cup milk, ½ cup cream, 1 vanilla bean, 4 yolks, ⅓ cup sugar
  • Sour cream ice cream: 1 cup sour cream, ½ cup milk, ⅓ cup sugar, lemon
  • Apple ribbons and powdered sugar

TECHNIQUE

  1. Caramelize the apples until golden but still holding their shape; cool.
  2. Warm the raisins in the Calvados and juice and leave to plump.
  3. Make the custard (to 180 °F) and the ice cream.
  4. Roll the apples and crumbs in thin buttered dough into slim cylinders 1½ inches thick. Bake at 400 °F for 15–18 minutes, until crisp.
  5. Cut the cylinders on the bias.
