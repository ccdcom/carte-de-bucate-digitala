Tarta de Santiago

Galician Almond Cake
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Galicia’s almond cake has been baked for centuries along the pilgrim road to Santiago de Compostela, where the apostle St. James is buried. It is made with only almonds, eggs, sugar and lemon, and dusted with powdered sugar around the stencil of the cross of the Order of Santiago.

Moist, fragrant and naturally free of wheat, it has been protected since 2010 with a European geographical indication, and pilgrims still celebrate the end of the Camino with a slice and a glass of sweet wine.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

The Pilgrim’s Almond Cake

Serves 8 · 1 hour
Four ingredients and a cross of powdered sugar: the cake every Galician bakery sells.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2½ cups finely ground blanched almonds (almond flour)
  • 1¼ cups sugar
  • 5 large eggs
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • ½ tsp ground cinnamon (optional)
  • Butter for the pan
  • Powdered sugar and a cross-shaped paper stencil

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 350 °F. Butter a 9-inch springform pan and line the base with parchment.
  2. Beat the eggs with the sugar for 3–4 minutes, until pale and slightly foamy.
  3. Fold in the almonds, lemon zest and cinnamon until evenly combined.
  4. Pour into the pan and bake for 35–40 minutes, until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.
  5. Cool completely in the pan.
  6. Set the cross stencil in the center, dust the whole cake with powdered sugar and lift the stencil carefully.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Santiago, Almond and Albariño

Serves 6 · 4 hours
A warm almond cake with a crisp top, Albariño-poached pear, almond milk ice cream and a sheet of almond praline.

INGREDIENTS

  • Cake: 1½ cups Marcona almond flour, ¾ cup sugar, 3 eggs, zest of ½ lemon and ½ orange, 2 tbsp brown butter
  • Pears: 3 small pears, 1½ cups Albariño, ⅓ cup sugar, 1 vanilla bean, 1 strip lemon peel
  • Almond milk ice cream: 2 cups almond milk, ½ cup cream, 4 yolks, ½ cup sugar, ¼ tsp almond extract
  • Praline: ½ cup toasted Marcona almonds, ½ cup sugar
  • Powdered sugar and a small cross stencil, lemon thyme

TECHNIQUE

  1. Ice cream: make a custard with the almond milk, cream, yolks and sugar to 180 °F, add the almond extract, chill and churn.
  2. Peel and halve the pears and poach them gently in the Albariño with the sugar, vanilla and peel for 20 minutes. Reduce the poaching liquid to a syrup.
  3. Praline: caramelize the sugar, add the almonds, pour onto a mat to cool, then break into shards (blend a little to powder).
  4. Make the cake batter as for the classic, with the zests and brown butter. Bake in 6 small buttered rings at 340 °F for 15–18 minutes, until set but very moist.
A warm small cake dusted with a tiny cross of powdered sugar, a fanned pear half glazed with Albariño syrup, a quenelle of almond milk ice cream on praline powder, a shard of praline and lemon thyme leaves.
