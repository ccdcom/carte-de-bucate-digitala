🇨🇭 Switzerland

Rösti

Swiss Potato Galette
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Rösti began as the breakfast of Bernese farmers: grated potatoes fried in butter or lard in a heavy pan until a golden crust formed on both sides, eaten before a long day in the fields.

It became a national symbol, so much that the cultural line between German- and French-speaking Switzerland is jokingly called the Röstigraben, the “rösti ditch.” Today it is served with eggs, with cheese, with bacon, or beside Zürich’s veal in cream sauce.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

The Bernese Farmhouse Rösti

Serves 4 · 45 minutes (plus boiling the day before)
Potatoes boiled the day before, grated and fried into a thick, golden cake.

INGREDIENTS

  • 2 lb waxy potatoes, boiled in their skins the day before and chilled
  • 4 tbsp butter or lard, divided
  • 1 tsp salt and black pepper
  • Optional: 3 oz bacon lardons, a sliced onion, or 4 fried eggs to serve

METHOD

  1. Peel the cold potatoes and grate them coarsely. Season with salt and pepper.
  2. If using bacon or onion, fry them first in the pan and mix them into the potatoes.
  3. Heat 2 tablespoons of butter in a 10-inch heavy skillet over medium heat. Add the potatoes and press lightly into an even cake.
  4. Cook for 12–15 minutes, shaking the pan, until a deep golden crust forms underneath.
  5. Slide the rösti onto a plate, add the rest of the butter to the pan, flip the rösti back in and cook the other side for 10 minutes.
  6. Serve cut into wedges, with fried eggs if you like.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Rösti, Crust and Cream

Serves 4 as a starter · 1½ hours
A thin, lacy, glass-crisp rösti with crème fraîche, Alpine cheese, smoked trout and chive.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1½ lb firm potatoes, raw, grated into fine threads and squeezed very dry
  • 4 tbsp clarified butter
  • ½ cup crème fraîche, seasoned with lemon and salt
  • 2 oz aged Gruyère or Sbrinz, finely grated
  • 4 oz hot-smoked trout or char, flaked
  • Trout roe, chives, mountain herbs (sorrel, chervil), pickled shallot

TECHNIQUE

  1. Rinse the potato threads, then wring them dry in a cloth. Season.
  2. Heat the clarified butter in a non-stick pan and spread thin, even 4-inch rounds of potato.
  3. Cook over medium heat for 6–7 minutes per side, pressing, until lacy, golden and glass-crisp. Drain on a rack.
  4. Top just before serving so they stay crisp.
A crisp rösti round, a quenelle of crème fraîche, flakes of smoked trout, trout roe, a snow of grated Alpine cheese, chive batons, mountain herbs and pickled shallot rings.
