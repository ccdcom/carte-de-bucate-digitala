Appeltaart

Dutch Apple Pie
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Dutch apple pie appears in a cookbook from 1514 and in the paintings of the Golden Age. Unlike the thin French tart, it is deep and generous: a buttery, crumbly crust filled high with apples, raisins and cinnamon under a lattice.

It is served in every Dutch café, a slice cut tall and always “met slagroom,” with a big spoon of whipped cream. Amsterdam’s Winkel 43 has queues every day for it.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Café Appeltaart

Serves 10 · 2 hours
A deep apple pie with cake-like crust and a lattice top, served with whipped cream.

INGREDIENTS

  • Dough: 2⅔ cups self-rising flour (or all-purpose with 2 tsp baking powder), 1 cup cold butter, ¾ cup brown sugar, 1 egg, pinch of salt
  • Filling: 3 lb tart apples (Goudrenet, Jonagold or Granny Smith), peeled and cut in chunks
  • ½ cup raisins, soaked in warm water
  • ¼ cup sugar, 2 tsp cinnamon, juice of ½ lemon
  • 2 tbsp breadcrumbs or crushed rusk
  • 1 egg yolk, beaten, to glaze
  • Whipped cream, to serve

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 350 °F. Butter a 9-inch springform pan.
  2. Mix the flour, butter, sugar, egg and salt into a soft dough. Keep a third aside for the lattice.
  3. Press the rest of the dough into the base and up the sides of the pan. Sprinkle the breadcrumbs on the bottom.
  4. Mix the apples with the drained raisins, sugar, cinnamon and lemon juice, and pile into the pan, pressing down.
  5. Roll the reserved dough into strips and make a lattice on top. Brush with egg yolk.
  6. Bake for 1 hour–1 hour 10 minutes, until deep golden. Cool in the pan.
  7. Serve at room temperature in tall slices with plenty of whipped cream.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Appeltaart, Caramel and Cinnamon

Serves 8 · 1 day
A slim apple tart of pressed caramelized apple layers, brown-butter crumble, Calvados cream and cinnamon ice cream.

INGREDIENTS

  • Pressed apples: 8 apples, sliced paper-thin, ½ cup sugar, 4 tbsp brown butter, 1 tsp cinnamon
  • Sablé base: 1 cup flour, ⅓ cup butter, ¼ cup brown sugar, 1 yolk, pinch of salt
  • Crumble: ½ cup flour, ¼ cup oats, 3 tbsp brown butter, 3 tbsp brown sugar
  • Calvados cream: ½ cup crème fraîche, 1 tbsp Calvados, 1 tbsp powdered sugar
  • Cinnamon ice cream: 1 cup milk, 1 cup cream, 4 yolks, ½ cup sugar, 2 cinnamon sticks
  • Apple caramel: ½ cup apple cider reduced with ¼ cup sugar

TECHNIQUE

  1. Layer the apple slices in a lined loaf pan, brushing each layer with brown butter and sprinkling with sugar and cinnamon. Cover and bake at 300 °F for 3 hours. Press under a weight overnight.
  2. Make the sablé dough, roll thin and bake at 340 °F until golden; cut into bars the size of the apple slices.
  3. Bake the crumble until golden and crisp.
  4. Make the ice cream: infuse the milk and cream with cinnamon, make a custard to 180 °F, chill and churn.
  5. Cut the pressed apple into neat bars, glaze with apple caramel and warm briefly.
