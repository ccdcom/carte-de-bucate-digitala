Koláče

Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Koláče are round pastries of soft yeast dough with a well of filling in the center: sweet farmer’s cheese, poppy seed, plum jam (povidla), apricot. Their name comes from kolo, “wheel” or “circle,” and they are baked for weddings, christenings and village feasts.

In Moravia the tradition is especially strong, with tiny wedding koláčky by the hundreds. Czech immigrants carried them to Texas, where towns like West still hold kolache festivals.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Village Wedding Koláče

Makes 16 · 2½ hours
Soft, buttery yeast buns with cheese, poppy seed and plum fillings, topped with crumble.

INGREDIENTS

  • Dough: 3½ cups all-purpose flour, 2¼ tsp instant yeast, ⅓ cup sugar, 1 cup warm milk, 2 egg yolks, ½ cup soft butter, ½ tsp salt, lemon zest
  • Cheese filling: 8 oz farmer’s cheese or quark, 1 yolk, 3 tbsp sugar, vanilla, raisins
  • Poppy seed filling: 1 cup ground poppy seeds simmered with ¾ cup milk and ¼ cup sugar until thick
  • Plum butter (povidla) or thick plum jam
  • Crumble: ½ cup flour, ¼ cup sugar, 3 tbsp butter
  • 1 egg, beaten, to glaze

METHOD

  1. Mix all the dough ingredients and knead for 8 minutes until smooth. Cover and let rise for 1 hour.
  2. Make the fillings and the crumble.
  3. Divide the dough into 16 balls and place on lined sheets. Flatten slightly and let rise 20 minutes.
  4. With the bottom of a small glass, press a deep well in the center of each, leaving a raised rim.
  5. Brush the rims with egg, fill the wells with a spoon of filling and sprinkle with crumble.
  6. Bake at 350 °F for 18–20 minutes, until golden. Brush with melted butter while warm.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Koláč, Plum and Poppy

Serves 6 · 4 hours
A warm brioche koláč with roasted plums, poppy seed cream, sweet quark ice cream and a rum caramel.

INGREDIENTS

  • Dough: a rich brioche dough (classic recipe with extra yolk and butter)
  • Plums: 6 Italian plums, halved, roasted with brown sugar, cinnamon and slivovice (plum brandy)
  • Poppy seed cream: ½ cup ground poppy seeds, ½ cup milk, 2 tbsp honey, ¼ cup whipped cream
  • Quark ice cream: 1 cup quark, ½ cup milk, ½ cup cream, ½ cup sugar, lemon zest
  • Rum caramel: ½ cup sugar, ¼ cup cream, 1 tbsp dark rum
  • Crumble, powdered sugar

TECHNIQUE

  1. Make and churn the quark ice cream.
  2. Cook the poppy seeds in milk and honey until thick, cool and fold in the whipped cream.
  3. Roast the plums at 375 °F for 15 minutes.
  4. Shape small koláče, fill with poppy cream and a roasted plum half, top with crumble and bake at 350 °F for 14 minutes.
  5. Make the rum caramel.
