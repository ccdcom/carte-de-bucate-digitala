Banitsa

Cheese Pie
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Banitsa is the bread and soul of the Bulgarian table: thin sheets of dough layered or coiled with eggs, yogurt and white brined cheese, baked until golden and crackling. It belongs to breakfast, to bakeries at dawn and to every celebration.

On New Year’s Eve it carries small fortunes folded inside, written on slips of paper wrapped around dogwood twigs, and whoever finds one learns what the coming year will bring. The family of Balkan pies stretches from the Turkish börek to Serbian gibanica, but banitsa is unmistakably Bulgarian.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Grandmother’s Coiled Banitsa

Serves 8 · 1¼ hours
Store-bought phyllo, sirene cheese and yogurt: the everyday banitsa of Bulgarian homes.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 lb phyllo dough, thawed
  • 14 oz Bulgarian sirene or feta, crumbled
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup plain whole-milk yogurt
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ½ cup butter, melted, mixed with ¼ cup sunflower oil
  • ½ cup sparkling water

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 375 °F and butter a 10-inch round pan.
  2. Stir the baking soda into the yogurt and let it foam for a few minutes. Beat in the eggs, then fold in the cheese.
  3. Lay one sheet of phyllo on the counter, brush with butter, cover with a second sheet and brush again.
  4. Spread a thin line of filling along the long edge and roll loosely into a long tube. Coil it in the center of the pan.
  5. Repeat with the remaining sheets, continuing the spiral outward until the pan is full.
  6. Drizzle the remaining butter over the top and sprinkle the sparkling water over it.
  7. Bake for 40–45 minutes, until deep golden. Cover with a clean towel for 10 minutes before cutting, so it softens slightly. Serve warm with yogurt or ayran.
Tip. The sparkling water is a Bulgarian trick: it makes the inner layers tender while the top stays crisp.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Banitsa, Brown Butter and Whey

Serves 4 · 3 hours
A single crisp spiral of hand-stretched dough, aged sirene custard, brown-butter foam and pickled green tomato.

INGREDIENTS

  • Dough: 1½ cups bread flour, ½ cup warm water, 1 tbsp sunflower oil, 1 tsp vinegar, ½ tsp salt
  • Filling: 7 oz aged sirene or barrel feta, 2 oz fresh sheep’s ricotta, 2 eggs, ⅓ cup thick yogurt
  • ½ cup clarified butter
  • Brown butter foam: 3 tbsp brown butter, ½ cup whole milk, ¼ cup whey (from strained yogurt), salt
  • Pickled green tomato: 1 green tomato, ¼ cup cider vinegar, 1 tbsp sugar
  • To finish: summer savory (chubritsa), toasted sunflower seeds

TECHNIQUE

  1. Knead the dough ingredients for 10 minutes until silky, oil, cover and rest for 1 hour.
  2. Blend the cheeses, eggs and yogurt into a smooth, thick custard.
  3. On a floured cloth, stretch the dough with the backs of your hands until translucent, about 30 inches across. Brush with clarified butter.
  4. Pipe a thin line of filling along one edge, roll into a pencil-thin tube and coil into four 4-inch spirals on a lined baking sheet. Brush with butter.
  5. Bake at 400 °F for 18–20 minutes, until glass-crisp and golden.
  6. Slice the green tomato paper-thin and steep in the warm vinegar and sugar for 20 minutes.
  7. Warm the milk, whey and brown butter to 140 °F, season and froth with a hand blender just before serving.
One spiral per plate, a spoon of brown-butter foam to one side, three folded slices of pickled green tomato, a few toasted sunflower seeds and leaves of savory.
