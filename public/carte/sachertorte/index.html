Sachertorte

Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

In 1832 Prince Metternich asked his kitchen for a new dessert. The head chef was ill, and 16-year-old apprentice Franz Sacher created a chocolate cake with apricot jam and a dark chocolate glaze. His son Eduard later perfected it, and the Hotel Sacher made it world famous.

A long court battle with the Demel pastry shop over the right to the name “Original Sachertorte” ended in 1963. The cake is always served with unsweetened whipped cream (Schlagobers) and a Viennese coffee.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

The Viennese Coffeehouse Cake

Serves 12 · 2½ hours, plus setting
Dense chocolate sponge, apricot jam and a shiny chocolate glaze.

INGREDIENTS

  • Cake: 5 oz dark chocolate, ½ cup + 2 tbsp butter, soft, ½ cup powdered sugar, 6 eggs, separated, ½ cup sugar, 1 cup all-purpose flour, 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 cup apricot jam, warmed and strained, with 1 tbsp rum
  • Glaze: 1 cup sugar, ½ cup water, 7 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • Lightly whipped unsweetened cream, to serve

METHOD

  1. Heat the oven to 350 °F. Line a 9-inch springform pan.
  2. Melt the chocolate and let it cool. Beat the butter with the powdered sugar until fluffy, add the yolks one by one, then the chocolate and vanilla.
  3. Whip the whites, adding the sugar gradually, to soft peaks. Fold into the chocolate mixture alternately with the flour.
  4. Bake for 50–60 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean. Cool upside down on a rack.
  5. Cut the cake in half horizontally, spread with warm jam and reassemble. Brush the top and sides with jam and let it set for 1 hour.
  6. Glaze: boil the sugar and water for 5 minutes, cool slightly, then stir in the chocolate until smooth and glossy (about 230 °F, it should coat a spoon).
  7. Pour the glaze over the cake in one go and smooth with one or two strokes of a palette knife. Let it set at room temperature. Serve with whipped cream.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Sacher, Apricot and Cream

Serves 8 · 1 day
A slim slice of Sacher sponge, apricot in three textures, a dark chocolate glaze and a cold Schlagobers foam.

INGREDIENTS

  • Sponge: the classic Sacher batter, baked in a thin sheet
  • Apricot: 8 ripe apricots (poached), apricot purée gel, apricot jam with Marillenschnaps
  • Glaze: 5 oz dark chocolate (70%), ½ cup cream, 2 tbsp glucose, 1 tbsp butter
  • Schlagobers foam: 1 cup heavy cream, a little vanilla, in a siphon or lightly whipped
  • Cocoa crumble, gold leaf (optional)

TECHNIQUE

  1. Bake the sponge thin, cut into 3 equal strips and layer with apricot jam. Press and chill, then cut into slim bars.
  2. Glaze the bars with the warm chocolate glaze and let set.
  3. Poach apricot halves in light syrup; make a gel from apricot purée with agar.
  4. Charge the siphon with cold cream or whip it just to soft peaks.
A glossy Sacher bar, two poached apricot halves, dots of apricot gel, a little cocoa crumble and a cloud of cold Schlagobers foam alongside.
