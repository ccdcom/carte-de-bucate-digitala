Paella Valenciana

Valencian Rice Dish
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Paella was born in the rice fields around Lake Albufera near Valencia, cooked by farm workers over orange-wood fires in a wide, shallow pan, the paella that gave the dish its name. The original had no seafood: chicken, rabbit, flat green beans, garrofó beans, saffron and sometimes snails.

The rice is never stirred once the broth is in, and the prize is the socarrat, the crisp, toasted layer at the bottom of the pan. Seafood and mixed paellas came later, along the coast, but Valencians are clear about which is the real one.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Sunday Paella Valenciana

Serves 6 · 1½ hours
Chicken, rabbit, beans and saffron, cooked outdoors or on the widest burner you have.

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 lb chicken thighs and 1 lb rabbit, in pieces (or all chicken)
  • ½ cup olive oil
  • 8 oz flat green beans (or regular), cut in pieces
  • 4 oz garrofó or large lima beans
  • 1 ripe tomato, grated
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 2 cups Bomba or Calasparra rice
  • 6 cups hot water or light chicken stock
  • A pinch of saffron threads, 1 sprig rosemary, salt
  • Lemon wedges (optional)

METHOD

  1. Heat the oil in a 16-inch paella pan over medium-high heat. Season the meat and brown it slowly, 15 minutes, until golden on all sides.
  2. Push the meat to the edges, add the green beans and lima beans and fry for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the grated tomato and paprika to the center and cook 2 minutes.
  4. Pour in the hot water, add the saffron and salt, and simmer for 20 minutes to make a tasty broth. Taste: it should be slightly salty.
  5. Scatter the rice evenly across the pan and shake to level it. Do not stir from now on.
  6. Cook over high heat for 10 minutes, then lower the heat and cook 8–10 minutes more, until the rice is tender and the liquid absorbed. Lay the rosemary on top.
  7. For the socarrat, raise the heat for the last 1–2 minutes until you hear crackling and smell toasting. Rest 5 minutes under a towel.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Paella, Socarrat and Smoke

Serves 4 · 4 hours
A thin layer of Bomba rice in a reduced rabbit and chicken broth, cooked over vine wood, with rabbit loin, artichoke and alioli.

INGREDIENTS

  • Broth: rabbit and chicken carcasses, roasted, 1 onion, 1 tomato, garlic, saffron, 2 quarts water
  • 1 rabbit loin and 4 rabbit legs, deboned
  • 1¼ cups Bomba rice
  • ¼ cup olive oil, 1 tsp sweet paprika, 2 tbsp sofrito (tomato and garlic cooked down for 1 hour)
  • 4 baby artichokes, cleaned and halved
  • Flat green beans and garrofó beans, blanched
  • Saffron alioli: ½ cup alioli with a pinch of saffron infused in warm water
  • Rosemary flowers, lemon

TECHNIQUE

  1. Make a deep broth from the roasted carcasses and vegetables, simmering 3 hours. Strain and reduce to about 4 cups. Add the saffron.
  2. Brown the deboned legs in the pan, set aside. Fry the artichokes and beans, then the sofrito and paprika.
  3. Add the hot broth, bring to a boil and spread the rice in a very thin layer. Do not stir.
  4. Cook over vine-wood embers or high heat for 16–17 minutes, returning the legs on top halfway.
  5. Sear the rabbit loin and slice thickly.
  6. Finish the paella over the hottest point to build an even socarrat. Rest 3 minutes.
Served in the pan at the table: slices of rabbit loin laid on the rice, golden artichokes, a few rosemary flowers, dots of saffron alioli and lemon. Each guest scrapes the crisp socarrat from the bottom.
