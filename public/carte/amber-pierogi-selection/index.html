Amber Pierogi Selection

Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Pierogi have anchored Polish cooking since at least the 13th century. They traveled from village kitchens to noble manors and changed with region and season: potato and farmer’s cheese (ruskie) in the south, sauerkraut and wild mushroom in the forests, sweet berries in summer.

The “amber” selection reimagines three of them with thin, almost translucent dough and golden butter, like amber from the Baltic shore: familiar as a childhood memory, dressed for an evening out.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Grandmother’s Pierogi Ruskie

Makes about 40 · 2 hours
Potato and farmer’s cheese dumplings, boiled and topped with golden onions.

INGREDIENTS

  • Dough: 3½ cups all-purpose flour, 1 cup warm water, 3 tbsp melted butter or oil, 1 egg, 1 tsp salt
  • Filling: 1½ lb floury potatoes, boiled and mashed, 10 oz farmer’s cheese (twaróg), 2 onions, finely chopped and fried golden in butter, salt, plenty of black pepper
  • Topping: 1 onion, sliced and fried in butter until golden, or crisp bacon bits
  • Sour cream, to serve

METHOD

  1. Mix the dough ingredients and knead for 5–8 minutes until smooth and elastic. Cover and rest 30 minutes.
  2. Mix the mashed potatoes with the crumbled cheese and half of the fried onions. Season well with salt and pepper. Cool.
  3. Roll the dough thin (about 1/16 inch) and cut circles with a 3-inch cutter.
  4. Place a teaspoon of filling on each circle, fold in half and pinch the edges firmly to seal, crimping if you like.
  5. Cook in batches in a large pot of salted boiling water. When they float, cook 2 minutes more.
  6. Lift out with a slotted spoon and serve with fried onions and sour cream. Leftovers are excellent fried in butter the next day.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Three Ambers

Serves 4 (12 pierogi) · 4 hours
Three delicate pierogi: smoked potato and twaróg, wild mushroom and sauerkraut, and sour cherry, finished in amber brown butter.

INGREDIENTS

  • Dough: 2 cups “00” or all-purpose flour, ⅔ cup hot water, 2 tbsp butter, 1 yolk, salt; rolled very thin
  • Smoked potato filling: potatoes baked in the embers or smoked for 20 minutes, mashed with twaróg, caramelized onion, white pepper
  • Mushroom filling: dried porcini and fresh mushrooms with braised sauerkraut, finely chopped and reduced
  • Cherry filling: sour cherries with a little sugar and potato starch
  • ½ cup brown butter, crisp onion, chives, sour cream foam, mushroom jus, cherry reduction

TECHNIQUE

  1. Make the three fillings and chill them.
  2. Roll the dough until translucent and cut 2½-inch circles. Fill, seal and crimp each type with a different pattern.
  3. Boil in salted water until they float, plus 1 minute.
  4. Toss the savory ones in brown butter until lightly golden; keep the cherry ones glossy with a little butter and sugar.
Three pierogi on one plate, each with its partner: the smoked potato one with crisp onion and sour cream foam, the mushroom one in a spoon of dark mushroom jus, the cherry one with a few drops of cherry reduction. Brown butter spooned over all, and chives.
