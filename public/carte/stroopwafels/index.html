Stroopwafels

Dutch Caramel Waffles
Rustic version ↓ Michelin version ↓

The story behind the dish

Stroopwafels were first made in Gouda, the cheese town, in the late 18th or early 19th century. Bakers are said to have used leftover crumbs and spices, sweetened with syrup. Two thin waffles, baked crisp, split while hot and glued together with caramel syrup.

Fresh ones are sold warm at Dutch markets, the syrup still soft and stretching. At home, the Dutch rest one on top of a cup of coffee or tea so the steam softens the caramel.

THE RUSTIC VERSION

Gouda Market Stroopwafels

Makes about 12 · 1½ hours
Thin, spiced waffles split while hot and filled with cinnamon caramel.

INGREDIENTS

  • Dough: 2 cups all-purpose flour, 1 tsp instant yeast, ½ cup melted butter, ⅓ cup sugar, 1 egg, 3 tbsp warm milk, pinch of salt
  • Filling: ¾ cup dark brown sugar, ½ cup butter, ½ cup golden syrup (or light molasses), 1 tsp cinnamon
  • A pizzelle or thin waffle-cone iron

METHOD

  1. Mix the dough ingredients and knead into a soft dough. Cover and rise for 45 minutes.
  2. Make the filling: heat the sugar, butter, syrup and cinnamon, stirring, until smooth and just bubbling. Keep warm.
  3. Divide the dough into 12 balls.
  4. Heat the iron. Bake each ball for 1–1½ minutes, until golden.
  5. Immediately cut a round with a 4-inch cutter if needed, and slice it horizontally in half with a thin knife while hot.
  6. Spread one half with warm syrup, close with the other half and press. Cool flat.
Tip. Work fast: once the waffle cools, it cannot be split.
THE MICHELIN 3-STAR VERSION

Stroopwafel, Coffee and Cream

Serves 6 · 4 hours
A warm, freshly split stroopwafel, salted caramel ice cream, a coffee cream and a crisp cinnamon tuile.

INGREDIENTS

  • Stroopwafels: as in the classic recipe, made smaller (3-inch)
  • Caramel filling: as in the classic, with a pinch of fleur de sel
  • Salted caramel ice cream: ½ cup sugar, 1½ cups cream, 1 cup milk, 4 yolks, ½ tsp salt
  • Coffee cream: ½ cup mascarpone, ¼ cup cream, 1 tbsp espresso, 1 tbsp sugar
  • Cinnamon tuile: 2 tbsp butter, 2 tbsp sugar, 1 egg white, 2 tbsp flour, ½ tsp cinnamon
  • Gouda shavings (aged 2 years), optional

TECHNIQUE

  1. Ice cream: caramelize the sugar, add the warm cream and milk, make a custard with the yolks to 180 °F, add salt, chill and churn.
  2. Whip the coffee cream ingredients until just firm.
  3. Bake thin cinnamon tuiles at 340 °F for 6 minutes and shape over a rolling pin.
  4. Bake and fill the small stroopwafels just before serving.
A warm stroopwafel cut in half and stacked, a quenelle of salted caramel ice cream, a spoon of coffee cream, a curved cinnamon tuile and, for the brave, a few shavings of old Gouda.
