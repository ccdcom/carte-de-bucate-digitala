      Europe on a Plate

      A Culinary Journey Through Europe's Finest Destinations

      Europe on a Plate is an award-winning culinary journey through the cultures, traditions, flavors and destinations of Europe.

      Written by Christian Coandă and Mariana Caragicu Coandă, the book combines gastronomy, travel, history and cultural discovery into a unique exploration of the European continent.

      From traditional family recipes and regional specialties to the stories behind Europe's most memorable culinary experiences, Europe on a Plate offers readers an inspiring view of the people, places and traditions that shape European cuisine.

      As an added bonus, readers also gain access to a collection of complimentary recipes inspired by the regions featured in the book.

      About the Authors

      Christian Coandă and Mariana Caragicu Coandă are authors, travelers and culinary enthusiasts who share a passion for culture, history, food and discovery.

      Through books and digital projects, they aim to connect readers with the traditions and stories that make world cuisine one of humanity's richest cultural treasures.
